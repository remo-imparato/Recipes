# Caramel Macchiato

## Ingredients
- 2 Tbsp caramel sauce, divided
- 1 double shot espresso (1/4 cup)
- 1/2 cup milk, steamed with foam

## Directions
1. Add 1 Tbsp caramel sauce to the bottom of a mug that holds at least 8 oz.
2. Pour the espresso over the caramel, then add the hot milk. Spoon the remaining milk foam on top.
3. Drizzle the remaining 1 Tbsp caramel over the foam and serve promptly.

## Yield
1 serving (8 oz)

## Notes and Tips
A dedicated milk frother works best, though other methods can foam milk. Skim and whole milk are noted as best for producing plenty of bubbles; whole milk gives a richer, creamier drink. The source recommends fresh caramel sauce for the best flavor.

## Homemade Caramel Sauce

### Ingredients
- 1 cup granulated sugar
- 1/3 cup water
- 3/4 cup heavy whipping cream
- 2 Tbsp salted butter
- 1 tsp vanilla extract (optional)

### Directions
1. Add the sugar to a heavy-bottomed saucepan and shake the pan so the sugar forms an even layer.
2. Add enough water to moisten the sugar. Do not stir or move the pan after adding the water.
3. Cook over medium heat until the sugar dissolves and turns clear, a few minutes.
4. Increase the heat to medium-high and cook until the caramel turns amber, about 10 minutes.
5. Turn off the heat and immediately add the cream. The caramel may seize; this is expected.
6. Add the butter and stir until smooth. If it remains clumpy, return the pan to low heat and stir gently for a few minutes. Stir in the vanilla, if using.
7. Cool to room temperature, transfer to a jar, and refrigerate.

### Caramel Sauce Notes
- Yield: about 1 cup (8 servings). Prep: 5 minutes; cook: 10 minutes; total: 15 minutes.
- To add salt, stir in 1/2 tsp at the end with the vanilla. Unsalted butter may be used; start with 1/8 tsp salt and adjust to taste.
- With a thermometer, target 338-350 F. Above 350 F the caramel can taste burned and bitter; below 338 F it may not caramelize properly.
- Store airtight in the refrigerator for up to 1 month or freezer for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator or in a warm water bath.
- Reheat in a microwave-safe container in 15-second intervals, or in a saucepan over medium-low heat, until warm and pourable.
- To fix crystallized caramel, add 1/4 cup water and bring it back to a boil to dissolve the crystals, then continue cooking.
- Use a heavy-bottomed pan for even heat. Do not stir or move the pan after wetting the sugar until the cream is added. Once the cream is added, the fat helps prevent crystallization.
