Whipped Coffee (Instant)
Ingredients
	• 2 tablespoons instant coffee
	• 2 tablespoons granulated sugar, or to taste
	• 2 tablespoons boiling water
	• 1 cup milk of choice
Yield
Serves: 2
Preparation
In a medium bowl, combine instant coffee, sugar and boiling water. 

Rachael Ray Show
Whisk vigorously for 2 to 3 minutes in a zig-zag motion until mixture resembles whipped cream.

Rachael Ray Show
In a serving glass, add ice if serving cold and top with milk of choice or just add hot milk. Spoon whipped mixture on top, swirl into the milk and serve.
