# Minty Lemonade Iced Tea

A fresh twist on minty sweet iced tea.

## Ingredients
- 3 cups water
- 2 family-size tea bags
- 1 (1 oz) package fresh mint leaves (about 1 cup loosely packed)
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 4 cups cold water
- 1 (6 oz) can frozen lemonade concentrate, thawed
- Fresh citrus slices, for garnish

## Directions
1. Bring 3 cups water to a boil in a 2-qt saucepan. Remove from the heat, add the tea bags, and stir in the mint. Cover and steep for 10 minutes.
2. Remove and discard the tea bags and mint. Stir in the sugar until dissolved.
3. Pour the tea into a 3-qt container and stir in 4 cups cold water and the lemonade concentrate. Serve over ice and garnish, if desired.

## Variations
- **Bourbon-Lemonade Iced Tea:** Prepare as directed and stir in 1 cup bourbon. Yield: 9 cups.
- **Spiced Dark Rum-Lemonade Iced Tea:** Prepare as directed and stir in 1 cup spiced dark rum. Yield: 9 cups.

## Timing and Yield
Prep: 10 minutes; cook: 5 minutes; steep: 10 minutes; total: 25 minutes. Yield: 8 cups.
