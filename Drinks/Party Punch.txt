Party Punch
Every Southern host knows it's not a party without party punch.
By Julia Levy 
Published on February 20, 2024
Recipe tested by Southern Living Test Kitchen 
Active Time:
5 mins
Total Time:
5 mins
Servings:
12 
Ingredients
	• 1 (64-oz.) bottle cranberry-watermelon juice (such as Ocean Spray), chilled
	• 1 (12-oz.) can frozen limeade from concentrate, thawed (such as MinuteMaid)
	• 3/4 cup (6 oz.) vodka or white rum (optional)
	• 1 (1 liter) bottle ginger ale, chilled
	• 1 medium (5- to 6-oz.) orange, thinly sliced crosswise
	• 1 cup sliced fresh strawberries
	• Ice, for serving
Directions
	1.  Combine ingredients, and chill: 
Stir together cranberry-watermelon juice, limeade, and vodka, if using, in a large punch bowl or pitcher. If not already chilled, refrigerate until chilled, about 1 hour. 
	2. Add ginger ale and fruit: 
When ready to serve, add ginger ale, orange slices, and strawberries. 
Serve chilled with ice.
