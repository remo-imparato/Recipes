Frozen Caramel Coffee Slushies

Ingredients
2 SERVINGS
	
	• 1/4 cup Folgers Coffeehouse Blend Ground Coffee (strong brewed, chilled)
	
	• 1/4 cup Folgers Black Silk Coffee (strong brewed, chilled)
	
	• 2 Tbsp. Smucker's Sundae Syrup Caramel Flavored Syrup (plus additional for garnish)
	
	• 1/4 cup flavored coffee creamer (vanilla-)
	
	• 2 cups ice cubes 
	
	• whipped cream 
	
	• coarse sea salt
