Cherry Limeade
Cherry limeade is lemonade's sweetest competition. Plus, it's a drink the whole family can enjoy.
By Craig Ruff 
Published on July 14, 2024
Recipe tested by Southern Living Test Kitchen 
Active Time:
5 mins
Total Time:
5 mins
Servings:
10 
Ingredients
	• 1 (10-oz.) jar whole red maraschino cherries with stems, chilled
	• 1 (2-liter) bottle lemon-lime soft drink (such as Sprite), chilled
	• 6 limes, divided
	• 1/2 cup tart cherry juice, chilled
Directions
	1.  Drain cherries: 
Drain maraschino cherries, reserving syrup. Set cherries aside. 
	2. Juice limes: 
Juice 4 limes to yield 1/2 cup fresh juice; thinly slice remaining 2 limes. 
	3. Mix drink: 
Stir together maraschino cherry syrup, lemon-lime soft drink, lime juice, and tart cherry juice in a large pitcher. Add lime slices. 
	4. Pour over ice: 
Serve over ice garnished with reserved maraschino cherries.
