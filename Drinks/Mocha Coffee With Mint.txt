Mocha Coffee With Mint

Ingredients
	• 1 cup coffee
	• 1 cup whipping cream
	• 2 teaspoons mint syrup, divided
	• 2 teaspoons chocolate syrup
	• 1 sprig mint, for garnish
Preparation
While the coffee is brewing, whip up the whipped cream to soft peaks, fold in 1 teaspoon of mint syrup.

Once coffee has finished, mix in 2 teaspoons of chocolate syrup and 1 teaspoon of mint syrup. Top the coffee with the mint whipped cream and a sprig of mint before serving.
