Lemon-Mint Sparklers
Need a refreshing, company-friendly drink? Try these sparklers.
By Southern Living Test Kitchen 
Updated on April 16, 2024
Hands On Time:
30 mins
Total Time:
1 hr 30 mins
Yield:
1 1/2 qt.
Ingredients
	• 1 3/4 cups fresh lemon juice (about 10 lemons)
	• 1 1/2 cups Lemon-Mint Syrup (recipe below)
	• 3 1/2 cups chilled club soda
	• 2 cups ice cubes
Directions
	1. Stir together all ingredients in a large pitcher. Serve immediately. 
Lemon-Mint Syrup Recipe
	• Step 1. Using a vegetable peeler, remove thin layer of peel from 6 lemons, leaving white pith intact. Reserve peeled lemons for another use.
	• Step 2. Bring 2 cups granulated sugar, 2 tsp. kosher salt, and 4 cups water to a boil in a medium saucepan over medium-high heat, whisking occasionally. Remove syrup from heat, and stir in lemon peels and 6 to 8 sprigs of fresh mint. Cover and let stand 30 minutes. Pour mixture through a wire-mesh strainer into a container; discard solids. Refrigerate up to 2 weeks.
Makes about 4 cups.

 Variation 
Lemon-Mint-Bourbon Sparklers: Prepare recipe as directed, omitting lemon juice and reducing Lemon-Mint Syrup to 1/3 cup. Stir in 1 1/2 cups bourbon. Makes 5 1/3 cups. Hands-on 10 min.; Total 1 hour, 10 min, including syrup
