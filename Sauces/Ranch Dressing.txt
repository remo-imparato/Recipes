Ranch Dressing

Ingredients
	Mayonnaise (the source uses Vegenaise as a substitute)
	Plain yogurt or sour cream
	Onion powder
	Garlic powder
	Dried parsley
	Salt and pepper, to taste
	Water or milk, as needed to make a dressing

Directions
1. Decide how much dip or dressing to make.
2. Mix equal parts mayonnaise and yogurt.
3. Add a dash of onion powder, garlic powder, and parsley. For a large batch, use about 1 Tbsp or more of each; for a small batch, use about 1 tsp or more of each, to taste.
4. Add salt and pepper and mix well. At this stage, the mixture is a dip.
5. To make a dressing, add a few tablespoons of water or milk and stir. Add more until it reaches the desired consistency.
6. Serve with vegetables, salad, or ranch chicken.
