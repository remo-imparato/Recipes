Emerils Essence Powder

Ingredients
	1 Tbsp paprika
	1 Tbsp sea salt
	1 Tbsp garlic powder
	1 1/2 tsp onion powder
	1 1/2 tsp dried oregano
	1 1/2 tsp dried thyme
	1 tsp freshly ground black pepper
	1 tsp cayenne pepper (use more for extra heat)

Directions
1. Add all the ingredients to a small bowl and mix well. The source suggests using the back of a spoon to mash or grind them together.
2. Transfer to a 4-oz Mason jar or clean recycled spice jar.

Yield and Uses
Makes 1/3 cup. The source suggests using the blend with meat, poultry, seafood, grilled vegetables, or roasted vegetables. It also mentions Emeril Lagasse's original recipe on his website.
