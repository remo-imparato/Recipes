Sweet Curry Powder

	3 tsp turmeric
	2 tsp coriander
	1 1/2 tsp cumin
	1 tsp cinnamon
	1 tsp ginger
	3/4 tsp yellow mustard
	1/2 tsp white pepper (the source says it may be omitted if hard to find)
	1/2 tsp fenugreek
	1/2 tsp cardamom
	1/2 tsp cloves
	1/2 tsp nutmeg
	1/8 tsp cayenne pepper
	1/4 tsp black pepper
