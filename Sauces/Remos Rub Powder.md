# Remos Rub Powder

## Ingredients

### Braising mixture
- 1/2 cup white wine
- 1/2 cup Coke or root beer
- 2 Tbsp white wine vinegar
- 2 Tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 2 Tbsp honey
- 2 garlic cloves

### Hot rub
- 8 brown sugar
- 2 kosher salt
- 1.5 chili powder
- 1/4 mustard powder
- 1/4 paprika
- 1/4 cayenne
- 1/4 onion powder
- 1/4 garlic powder
- 1/4 Old Bay
- 1/4 black pepper
- 1/4 rubbed thyme
- 1/8 cinnamon
- 1/8 celery salt
- 1/2 oregano
- 1/4 ginger powder
- 1/8 nutmeg

### Rub
