BBQ Sauce

Ingredients
	2 cups ketchup
	1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
	1/4 cup Worcestershire sauce
	1/4 cup firmly packed brown sugar
	2 Tbsp molasses
	2 Tbsp mustard
	1 Tbsp Tabasco sauce or your favorite hot sauce
	1 Tbsp barbecue rub of your choice (optional)
	2 tsp liquid smoke (optional)
	1/2 tsp black pepper
	3 cloves fresh garlic, peeled
	2 chipotle peppers in adobo sauce, chopped (optional)

Directions
1. Combine all the ingredients except the garlic in a nonreactive saucepan and slowly bring to a boil.
2. Reduce the heat to low-medium and add the garlic cloves. Simmer for at least 15 minutes, then remove the garlic.
3. Transfer the sauce to clean jars and refrigerate. The source says it keeps for a couple of months.
