Homemade Ketchup
 
Homemade Ketchup is the perfect dip for your kids (or you) - no added sugar or funky ingredients, can be tweaked to your tastes, and it counts as a veggie!
Author: Brianne @ Cupcakes & Kale Chips, adapted from Fix Me a Snack's Homemade Ketchup
Recipe type: Dip, Condiment
Ingredients
	• 1 6 oz can tomato paste
	• ¼-1/2 cup cider vinegar (see Note)
	• ½ teaspoon salt
	• ½ teaspoon dried oregano
	• ½ teaspoon cumin
	• ⅛ teaspoon pepper
	• 1 teaspoon mustard powder
