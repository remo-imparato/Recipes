# Tart Cranberry Dipping Sauce (Alton Brown)

## Ingredients
- 1 pound frozen cranberries
- 2 cups orange juice
- 3 cups ginger ale
- 2 tablespoons maple syrup
- 2 tablespoons light brown sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- Zest of 1 orange

## Directions
1. Combine all the ingredients in a nonreactive saucepan and bring to a boil.
2. Lower the heat and simmer, stirring occasionally, for 30 to 45 minutes, until the liquid has reduced by about half.
3. Carefully blend until smooth. Taste and adjust the seasoning, then serve in small bowls.
