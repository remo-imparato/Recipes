# Ginger Scallion Dipping Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup fresh ginger, finely minced
- 1/2 cup green onions, finely sliced
- 1/2 cup neutral oil
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1/2 tsp salt

## Directions
1. Place the ginger, green onions, and salt in a heatproof bowl.
2. Heat the neutral oil until shimmering, then pour it over the aromatics.
3. Stir in the sesame oil and cool before serving with chicken or noodles.
