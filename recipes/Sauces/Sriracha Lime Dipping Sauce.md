# Sriracha Lime Dipping Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup sriracha
- 2 tbsp fresh lime juice
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 garlic clove, minced

## Directions
1. Whisk all ingredients together until smooth.
2. Taste and balance with more lime or honey.
3. Serve with shrimp, spring rolls, or fried appetizers.
