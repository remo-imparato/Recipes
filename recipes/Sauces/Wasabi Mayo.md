# Wasabi Mayo

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp wasabi paste
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 1/2 tsp rice vinegar
- 1/4 tsp garlic powder

## Directions
1. Stir the mayonnaise and wasabi together until smooth.
2. Mix in the soy sauce, vinegar, and garlic powder.
3. Taste and add more wasabi for heat; chill before serving.
