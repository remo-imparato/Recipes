# Coconut Curry Dipping Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup coconut milk
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- 1 tsp ginger, grated
- 1 garlic clove, minced

## Directions
1. Whisk all ingredients together in a saucepan.
2. Warm over medium-low heat until fragrant and slightly thickened.
3. Cool briefly and serve with shrimp, chicken, or vegetables.
