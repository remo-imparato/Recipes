# Vietnamese Nuoc Cham

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup fish sauce
- 2 tbsp fresh lime juice
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 1/2 cup warm water
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 Thai chili, thinly sliced

## Directions
1. Stir the sugar into the warm water until dissolved.
2. Add the fish sauce, lime juice, garlic, and chili.
3. Taste and balance with more lime, sugar, or water before serving.
