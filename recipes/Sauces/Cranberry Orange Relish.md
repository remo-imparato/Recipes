# Cranberry Orange Relish

## Ingredients
- 12 ounces cranberries
- 1 orange
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 tablespoon orange zest

## Directions
1. Zest the orange, then remove any seeds and cut it into chunks.
2. Pulse the cranberries, orange chunks, sugar, and zest in a food processor until finely chopped.
3. Cover and chill for at least 2 hours before serving.
