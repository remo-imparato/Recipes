# Cranberry, Apple, Orange & Onion Cranberry Sauce

## Ingredients
- 12 oz (340 g) fresh cranberries
- 1 medium apple, peeled and diced small (Honeycrisp, Gala, or Fuji)
- ½ medium sweet onion, finely diced
- 1 large orange
- ¾ cup brown sugar
- ¼ cup maple syrup (optional)
- 1 tablespoon water, for cooking the onion
- ¼ cup water
- ¼ teaspoon cinnamon
- Pinch of nutmeg
- Pinch of salt

## Directions
1. Zest the orange, then squeeze out the juice. Set aside.
2. In a saucepan over medium heat, cook the diced onion with a tablespoon of water for 5–7 minutes until softened and translucent. Do not let it brown.
3. Add the cranberries, apple, orange juice, orange zest, brown sugar, ¼ cup water, cinnamon, nutmeg, and salt.
4. Bring to a gentle boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for 12–15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
5. As the cranberries burst and the apples soften, stir in the maple syrup, if using.
6. Continue cooking until the sauce reaches your desired consistency. It will thicken considerably as it cools.
7. Remove from the heat and let cool to room temperature before refrigerating.

## Notes
- For a chunkier sauce, mash only some of the cranberries.
- For more orange flavor, add a few thin strips of candied orange peel.
- For extra holiday warmth, add 1 tablespoon bourbon during the last few minutes of cooking.
- This tastes even better after sitting overnight in the refrigerator.

Makes about 2½ cups. Serve with turkey, ham, pork loin, or leftover turkey sandwiches.
