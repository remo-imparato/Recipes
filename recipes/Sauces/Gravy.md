# Gravy

## Ingredients
- 2 Tbsp butter
- 2 Tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2 cups liquid (beef/chicken broth, pan drippings, etc.)
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce (optional)
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Directions
1. Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat.
2. Whisk in the flour and cook for 1-2 minutes, stirring constantly. It should turn a light golden brown.
3. Slowly pour in the broth while whisking to prevent lumps.
4. Add the Worcestershire sauce, if using.
5. Simmer for 5-10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the gravy thickens.
6. Season with salt and pepper to taste.

## Quick Tips
- Too thick? Add a little more broth.
- Too thin? Simmer a few minutes longer, or whisk 1 Tbsp cornstarch with 1 Tbsp cold water and stir it in.
- More flavor? Add a pinch of garlic powder, onion powder, or a teaspoon of beef bouillon.
