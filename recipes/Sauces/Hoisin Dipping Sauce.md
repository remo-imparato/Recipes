# Hoisin Dipping Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup hoisin sauce
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 2 tbsp water

## Directions
1. Whisk all ingredients until smooth.
2. Thin with additional water as needed.
3. Serve with lettuce wraps, spring rolls, or roasted meats.
