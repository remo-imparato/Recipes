# Sesame Soy Dipping Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1/2 tsp ginger, grated
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- 1 green onion, chopped

## Directions
1. Whisk the soy sauce, sesame oil, vinegar, honey, garlic, and ginger.
2. Stir in the sesame seeds and green onion.
3. Let stand for 5 minutes before serving.
