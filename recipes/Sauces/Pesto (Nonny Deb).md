# Pesto (Nonny Deb)

## Ingredients
- 2 cups fresh basil leaves, tightly packed
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 black pepper (optional; the source gives no unit, likely 1/2 tsp)
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1/2 cup finely chopped pine nuts or walnuts
- 1/4-3/4 cup olive oil
- 1/2 cup Parmesan cheese
