# Spicy Mayo Dipping Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp sriracha
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1/2 tsp honey

## Directions
1. Whisk all ingredients until smooth.
2. Taste and adjust the sriracha and honey.
3. Chill until ready to serve with sushi, fries, or vegetables.
