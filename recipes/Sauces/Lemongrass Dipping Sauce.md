# Lemongrass Dipping Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup water
- 1/4 cup rice vinegar
- 1/4 cup sugar
- 1 stalk lemongrass, finely minced
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 Thai chili, sliced
- 1 tbsp fish sauce

## Directions
1. Combine the water, vinegar, sugar, and lemongrass in a saucepan.
2. Simmer until the sugar dissolves, then cool.
3. Stir in the garlic, chili, and fish sauce before serving.
