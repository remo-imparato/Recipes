# Honey Garlic Dipping Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup honey
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tsp ginger, grated
- 1 tsp sesame oil

## Directions
1. Whisk the honey, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, ginger, and sesame oil in a saucepan.
2. Warm gently until fragrant; do not boil.
3. Cool and serve with fried appetizers or grilled chicken.
