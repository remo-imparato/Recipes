# Korean BBQ Dipping Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 tsp gochujang
- 1 green onion, chopped

## Directions
1. Whisk all ingredients together until the honey dissolves.
2. Adjust the gochujang to taste.
3. Serve with Korean barbecue, lettuce wraps, or grilled vegetables.
