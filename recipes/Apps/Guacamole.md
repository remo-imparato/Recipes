# Guacamole

## Ingredients
- 3 ripe avocados
- Juice of 1 lime
- 1/4 cup grated onion
- 1 small jalapeño, seeded and minced
- 2 tbsp chopped cilantro
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt, or to taste
- 1 small Roma tomato, diced (optional)

## Directions
1. Mash the avocados in a bowl, leaving some chunks for texture.
2. Stir in the lime juice.
3. Add the onion, jalapeño, cilantro, salt, and tomato if using.
4. Taste and adjust the salt and lime as needed.
5. Let sit for 10 minutes before serving to allow the flavors to meld.

## Notes
For grilled meats, add 1/2 teaspoon cumin and a pinch of chipotle powder. This version is especially good with pork, chicken, or ribs.
