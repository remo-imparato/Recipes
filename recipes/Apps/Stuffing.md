# Stuffing

A traditional holiday stuffing made with toasted bread cubes, sautéed onions, celery, and butter, seasoned with poultry seasoning, sage, thyme, and black pepper. Chicken broth moistens the bread, which bakes golden brown on top with a tender, flavorful center.

## Ingredients
- Day-old bread, cubed and dried
- Butter
- Onion, diced
- Celery, diced
- Chicken broth
- Poultry seasoning
- Dried sage
- Dried thyme
- Salt and pepper

## Directions
1. Dry or toast the bread cubes until firm.
2. Sauté the onion and celery in butter until softened.
3. Combine the bread cubes with the vegetables and seasonings.
4. Gradually add chicken broth until the mixture is moist but not soggy.
5. Transfer to a buttered baking dish.
6. Cover and bake until heated through, then uncover and bake until the top is golden brown.

**Family Note:** A classic holiday side dish that pairs perfectly with turkey, chicken, or roast pork. Adjust the broth to achieve your preferred texture, from soft and moist to lightly crisp.

## To Cook Stuffing in a Turkey
1. Prepare the stuffing as directed, but keep it slightly less moist than if baking separately.
2. Loosely spoon the stuffing into the neck and body cavities of the turkey just before roasting. Do not pack tightly, as the stuffing expands during cooking.
3. Truss or secure the openings as desired and roast the turkey according to your preferred turkey recipe.
4. For food safety, the center of the stuffing must reach 165°F (74°C). Check the center of the stuffing in both cavities with an instant-read thermometer.
5. If the turkey is finished before the stuffing reaches 165°F, remove the stuffing to a baking dish and continue baking until it reaches that temperature.
6. Let the turkey rest for 20–30 minutes before removing and serving the stuffing.

**Cook's Note:** Stuffing cooked inside the turkey absorbs flavorful juices from the bird, producing a richer taste and softer texture than stuffing baked separately. Bake any excess stuffing in a covered casserole dish during the last 30–40 minutes of roasting, until its center reaches 165°F (74°C).
