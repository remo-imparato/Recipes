# Thai Peanut Dipping Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup creamy peanut butter
- 1/4 cup coconut milk
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp lime juice
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tsp red curry paste
- 1 garlic clove, minced

## Directions
1. Whisk the peanut butter, coconut milk, soy sauce, lime juice, sugar, curry paste, and garlic in a saucepan.
2. Warm over low heat until smooth, adding water if needed.
3. Cool slightly and serve with satay or fresh vegetables.
