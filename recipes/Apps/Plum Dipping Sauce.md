# Plum Dipping Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup plum preserves
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 1/2 tsp sesame oil
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes

## Directions
1. Combine all ingredients in a small saucepan.
2. Warm over low heat, stirring until smooth.
3. Cool before serving with egg rolls, wontons, or roasted duck.
