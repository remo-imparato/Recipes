# Sweet Chili Dipping Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup water
- 1/2 cup rice vinegar
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 red chili, finely chopped
- 1 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp water

## Directions
1. Combine the water, vinegar, sugar, garlic, and chili in a saucepan.
2. Bring to a simmer and stir until the sugar dissolves.
3. Whisk in the cornstarch slurry and simmer until glossy and thickened.
4. Cool before serving with spring rolls, dumplings, or grilled foods.
