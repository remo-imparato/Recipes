# Oyster Sauce Dipping Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup oyster sauce
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp sugar
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes
- 2 tbsp water

## Directions
1. Whisk all ingredients together until the sugar dissolves.
2. Add water to thin to the desired consistency.
3. Serve with dumplings, vegetables, or grilled meat.
