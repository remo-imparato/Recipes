# Tamarind Dipping Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup tamarind concentrate
- 1/4 cup water
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp fish sauce
- 1/2 tsp chili flakes
- 1 tbsp lime juice

## Directions
1. Whisk the tamarind, water, sugar, fish sauce, and chili flakes in a saucepan.
2. Simmer until the sugar dissolves and the sauce thickens slightly.
3. Stir in the lime juice and cool before serving.
