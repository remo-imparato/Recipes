# Dumpling Dipping Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tbsp black vinegar or rice vinegar
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp chili oil
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 green onion, sliced

## Directions
1. Combine the soy sauce, vinegar, sesame oil, and chili oil.
2. Stir in the garlic and green onion.
3. Serve immediately with steamed or pan-fried dumplings.
