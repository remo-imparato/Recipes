# Ponzu Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup lemon juice
- 1/4 cup lime juice
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp mirin
- 1 tsp bonito flakes
- 1/2 tsp ginger, grated

## Directions
1. Whisk the soy sauce, citrus juices, vinegar, and mirin together.
2. Stir in the bonito flakes and ginger.
3. Let stand for 30 minutes, then strain if desired and serve chilled.
