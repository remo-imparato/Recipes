# Eggplant Parmigiana

## Ingredients
- Eggplant

## Directions
1. Slice the eggplant horizontally and lay the circles on paper towels.
2. Salt them to release water.
3. Pat dry and dip in flour on both sides.
4. Put a layer of sauce on the bottom of a baking dish and arrange the eggplant in the dish, alternating eggplant layers with sauce layers.
5. Top with cheese.
