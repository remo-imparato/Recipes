# Soy Ginger Dipping Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp fresh ginger, grated
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 green onion, thinly sliced

## Directions
1. Whisk the soy sauce, vinegar, sesame oil, and honey together.
2. Stir in the ginger, garlic, and green onion.
3. Rest for 5 minutes, then serve with dumplings or vegetables.
