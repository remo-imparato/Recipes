# Teriyaki Dipping Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup soy sauce
- 1/4 cup water
- 2 tbsp brown sugar
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 tsp ginger, grated
- 1 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp water

## Directions
1. Combine the soy sauce, water, sugar, honey, garlic, and ginger in a saucepan.
2. Bring to a simmer and whisk in the cornstarch slurry.
3. Cook until thick and glossy, then cool before serving.
