# Gochujang Dipping Sauce

## Ingredients
- 1/2 cup gochujang
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tbsp honey
- 1 garlic clove, minced
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1/2 tsp ginger, grated
- 2 tbsp water

## Directions
1. Whisk all ingredients together until smooth.
2. Add more water if needed to reach a dipping consistency.
3. Serve with Korean pancakes, vegetables, or grilled meat.
