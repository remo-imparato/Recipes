# Black Bean Soup (Nonny Deb)

## Ingredients
- 1 lb dried black beans, or 2 cans black beans
- Water, enough to cover dried beans by 1 inch, plus additional water as needed (amount not specified)
- 1 onion
- 1 green pepper (the directions refer to red pepper)
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1 Tbsp cumin
- 1 Tbsp oregano
- Ham hock (optional)
- 1/2 cup olive oil
- 1/3 cup vinegar, added during the last 15 minutes

## Directions
1. If using dried beans, cover them with water by 1 inch and cook for 4-5 hours. Canned beans are offered as an alternative.
2. The source says to puree the onion, pepper, and garlic, then add water and the ham hock, if using. It does not specify when to puree or add these to the beans, nor does it give complete cooking directions for the canned-bean option.
3. Add the vinegar during the last 15 minutes of cooking.

## Note
The source calls for a green pepper in the ingredient list but a red pepper in the directions; this discrepancy is retained.
