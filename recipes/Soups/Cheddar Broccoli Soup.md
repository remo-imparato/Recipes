# Cheddar Broccoli Soup

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup butter
- 1/2 medium onion, chopped (about 1/2 cup)
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 2 cups half-and-half cream (or heavy cream)
- 2 cups low-sodium chicken stock or broth
- 1/2 lb broccoli (about 3 cups), chopped into bite-size pieces
- 1 cup matchstick carrots
- 8 ounces sharp cheddar cheese, grated (2 cups)
- 1/8 teaspoon nutmeg (optional)
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Directions
1. Melt the butter in a pot over medium heat. Add the onion and sauté until soft.
2. Sprinkle the flour over the onion mixture. Cook and stir over medium heat for 1–2 minutes.
3. Whisk in the half-and-half and chicken broth.
4. Add the broccoli and carrots. Cook over medium-low heat for 20–25 minutes, or until the broccoli and carrots are tender.
5. Leave the soup chunky, or blend about 1 cup with an immersion blender and stir it back in for a smoother soup.
6. Remove the soup from the heat and stir in the cheese. Avoid adding the cheese over high heat, which can make the soup grainy.
7. Stir in the nutmeg, if desired, and season with salt and pepper to taste.
