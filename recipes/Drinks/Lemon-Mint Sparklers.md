# Lemon-Mint Sparklers

A refreshing, company-friendly drink.

## Ingredients
- 1 3/4 cups fresh lemon juice (about 10 lemons)
- 1 1/2 cups Lemon-Mint Syrup (recipe below)
- 3 1/2 cups chilled club soda
- 2 cups ice cubes

## Directions
Stir all ingredients together in a large pitcher. Serve immediately.

## Lemon-Mint Syrup
### Ingredients
- Peel from 6 lemons, removed in thin strips with a vegetable peeler
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- 2 tsp kosher salt
- 4 cups water
- 6-8 sprigs fresh mint

### Directions
1. Use a vegetable peeler to remove a thin layer of peel from 6 lemons, leaving the white pith intact. Reserve the peeled lemons for another use.
2. Bring the sugar, salt, and water to a boil in a medium saucepan over medium-high heat, whisking occasionally. Remove from the heat and stir in the lemon peels and mint. Cover and steep for 30 minutes.
3. Pour through a wire-mesh strainer into a container and discard the solids. Refrigerate for up to 2 weeks.

Yield: about 4 cups syrup.

## Variation: Lemon-Mint-Bourbon Sparklers
Prepare the syrup as directed, then omit the lemon juice and reduce the syrup to 1/3 cup. Stir in 1 1/2 cups bourbon. Yield: 5 1/3 cups. Hands-on time: 10 minutes; total time: 1 hour 10 minutes, including the syrup.

## Timing
Hands-on time: 30 minutes; total time: 1 hour 30 minutes.
