# Frozen Caramel Coffee Slushies

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup Folgers Coffeehouse Blend ground coffee, strongly brewed and chilled
- 1/4 cup Folgers Black Silk coffee, strongly brewed and chilled
- 2 Tbsp Smucker's Sundae Syrup caramel-flavored syrup, plus more for garnish
- 1/4 cup flavored coffee creamer (vanilla)
- 2 cups ice cubes
- Whipped cream
- Coarse sea salt

## Directions
The source does not include preparation directions.

## Yield
2 servings.
