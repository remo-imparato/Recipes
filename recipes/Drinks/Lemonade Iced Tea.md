# Lemonade Iced Tea

Why have one drink when you can have two?

## Ingredients
- 7 cups cold water, divided
- 2 family-size (10 oz each) black tea bags (such as Luzianne)
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 cup granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup fresh lemon juice (from 3 lemons)

## Directions
1. Bring 5 cups of water to a boil in a medium saucepan over high heat. Remove the pan from the heat and stir in the tea bags and baking soda. Steep for 10 minutes.
2. Remove and discard the tea bags. Pour the tea into a small heatproof pitcher. Stir in the sugar, lemon juice, and remaining 2 cups water until the sugar dissolves, about 1 minute. Serve over ice.

## Timing and Yield
- Active time: 15 minutes
- Total time: 25 minutes
- Serves: 4
