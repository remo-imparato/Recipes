# Cherry Limeade

Cherry limeade is lemonade's sweetest competition, and a drink the whole family can enjoy.

## Ingredients
- 1 (10 oz) jar whole red maraschino cherries with stems, chilled
- 1 (2 L) bottle lemon-lime soft drink (such as Sprite), chilled
- 6 limes, divided
- 1/2 cup chilled tart cherry juice

## Directions
1. Drain the maraschino cherries, reserving the syrup. Set the cherries aside.
2. Juice 4 limes to yield 1/2 cup juice. Thinly slice the remaining 2 limes.
3. Stir the cherry syrup, lemon-lime soft drink, lime juice, and tart cherry juice together in a large pitcher. Add the lime slices.
4. Serve over ice, garnished with the reserved cherries.

## Timing and Yield
- Active time: 5 minutes
- Total time: 5 minutes
- Serves: 10
