# Watermelon-Basil Sweet Tea

Homemade watermelon syrup gives this sweet tea a summery freshness.

## Ingredients
- 2 family-size tea bags
- 1/4 cup loosely packed fresh basil leaves (from one 0.5 oz package)
- 1/4 tsp baking soda
- 5 1/2 cups water, divided
- 3/4 cup Watermelon Simple Syrup, plus more if needed
- Basil sprigs, for garnish
- Watermelon slices, for garnish

## Directions
1. Place the tea bags, basil leaves, and baking soda in a large glass jar or pitcher.
2. Bring 4 cups water to a rolling boil in a medium saucepan over high heat. Immediately pour it over the tea bags and basil, making sure they are submerged. Steep for 15 minutes.
3. Remove the basil and tea bags, gently squeezing them with kitchen tongs; discard them. Stir in the Watermelon Simple Syrup and remaining 1 1/2 cups water. Add more syrup to taste, if desired.
4. Chill uncovered until cold, about 1-2 hours. Serve over ice, garnished with basil sprigs and watermelon slices. Store covered in the refrigerator for up to 2 days.

## Timing and Yield
- Active time: 20 minutes
- Total time: 1 hour 40 minutes
- Serves: 4

## Note
The source refers to Watermelon Simple Syrup but does not include its recipe here.
