# Honey’s Macaroni

## Ingredients
- 1 lb bacon
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 3 cups elbow macaroni
- 2 1/2 cans tomato soup
- 1 can water
- 1 block cheddar cheese, cubed

## Directions
1. Fry the bacon and crumble it into small pieces. Saute the onion.
2. Cook the macaroni.
3. Combine all ingredients in a large mixing bowl, then transfer to a casserole pan.
4. Bake uncovered at 350 F for 30-40 minutes.
