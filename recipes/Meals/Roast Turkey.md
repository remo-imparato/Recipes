# Roast Turkey

## Ingredients
### Brine
- 1 (14- to 16-pound) young turkey, thawed, giblets removed
- 1 gallon vegetable broth
- 1 cup kosher salt
- 1/2 cup light brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon black peppercorns
- 1 1/2 teaspoons allspice berries
- 1 1/2 teaspoons chopped candied ginger
- 1 gallon ice water

### For roasting
- 1 red apple, quartered
- 1/2 yellow onion, quartered
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 1 cup water
- 4 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 6 fresh sage leaves
- Canola oil

## Directions

### Brining
1. In a large pot, bring the broth, salt, brown sugar, peppercorns, allspice, and ginger to a boil, stirring to dissolve the salt and sugar. Cool completely, then chill.
2. Combine the cold brine with the ice water in a food-safe container large enough to hold the turkey. Submerge the turkey and keep it refrigerated at 40°F or colder for 8 to 16 hours, turning it once halfway through. Do not leave the turkey at room temperature while brining.
3. Remove the turkey from the brine, discard the brine, and pat the turkey dry.

### Roasting
1. Heat the oven to 500°F.
2. Put the apple, onion, cinnamon stick, and water in a microwave-safe dish. Microwave for 5 minutes, then place the solids in the turkey cavity with the rosemary and sage. Tuck the wings under the bird and lightly coat the skin with oil.
3. Roast on a rack in a roasting pan for 30 minutes. 
4. Reduce the oven to 350°F and continue roasting until a thermometer in the thickest part of the breast and innermost thigh reads 165°F, about 1 1/2 to 2 hours more. If the skin browns too quickly, cover it loosely with foil.
5. Rest the turkey for at least 15 minutes before carving.

## Variation: Herb-Roasted Turkey

### Ingredients
- 1 (12- to 14-pound) turkey, thawed, giblets removed
- 1/2 cup butter, softened
- 4 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tablespoon each chopped rosemary, thyme, and sage
- Salt and black pepper, to taste
- 1 cup broth

### Directions
1. Heat the oven to 325°F.
2. Mix the butter, garlic, rosemary, thyme, sage, salt, and pepper. Carefully loosen the skin and rub some of the butter mixture underneath it, then rub the rest over the outside of the turkey.
3. Place the turkey on a rack in a roasting pan and pour the broth into the pan. Roast for about 3 to 3 1/2 hours, until a thermometer in the thickest part of the breast and innermost thigh reads 165°F. Cooking time can vary.
4. Rest the turkey for 30 minutes before carving.
