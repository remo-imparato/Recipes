# Spanish Rice

## Ingredients
- 1/2 lb ground beef
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 1/2 green pepper, diced
- 1 cup white Minute Rice
- 1 large can tomato sauce
- About 1/2 can water

## Directions
1. Brown the ground beef, onion, and green pepper in a deep frying pan.
2. Add the rice, tomato sauce, and about 1/2 can of water.
3. Cover and simmer for 20-30 minutes, stirring often.
