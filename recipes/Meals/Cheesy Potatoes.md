# Cheesy Potatoes

## Ingredients
- 1 package cubed frozen hash browns (thawed)
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 1 package shredded cheddar cheese
- 1 (12 oz) container sour cream
- 1/2 diced onion (optional)
- 1/2 stick real butter
- Regular potato chips, for topping

## Directions
1. Saute the onion in a pan with the butter.
2. Mix the hash browns, soup, cheese, sour cream, and onion mixture in a bowl, then place in a casserole dish.
3. Cover and bake at 350 F for 45 minutes.
4. Uncover, sprinkle crushed potato chips on top, and bake for 15 more minutes.
