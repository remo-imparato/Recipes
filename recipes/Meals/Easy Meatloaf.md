# Easy Meatloaf

## Ingredients
- 1 lb ground beef
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 1 tsp mustard
- 1 cup milk
- 1 cup Italian bread crumbs
- 1 egg
- 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce (the source says "Worcester sauce")
- 1 lb breakfast sausage

### Sauce
- 1/2 cup ketchup
- 1/2 cup brown sugar

## Directions
1. Mix all ingredients except the sauce ingredients and shape into a loaf pan.
2. Mix the ketchup and brown sugar in a small bowl and glaze the meatloaf.
3. Bake at 375 F for 1 1/2 hours.
