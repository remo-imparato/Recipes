# Mashed Potatoes

## Ingredients
- 2 pounds Yukon gold potatoes
- 4 garlic cloves, peeled
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 4 ounces unsalted butter, cut into pieces
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

## Directions
1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into even 1/2-inch pieces. Put them and the garlic in a saucepan, cover with cold water by at least an inch, and bring to a boil.
2. Uncover, lower the heat to a simmer, and cook for 15 to 20 minutes, until the potatoes and garlic are very tender. Drain.
3. In the empty saucepan, warm the cream, butter, salt, and pepper until the butter melts. Remove from the heat.
4. Mash the potatoes and garlic, or pass them through a food mill, then stir gently into the cream mixture. Taste and adjust the seasoning.
