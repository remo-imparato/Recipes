# Good Eats Thanksgiving Dinner (Alton Brown)

Alton Brown's Thanksgiving menu: roast turkey, creamy mashed potatoes, sweet corn bread pudding, and tart cranberry dipping sauce.

## Good Eats Roast Turkey

### Ingredients
- 1 (14- to 16-pound) young turkey, thawed, giblets removed
- 1 gallon vegetable broth
- 1 cup kosher salt
- 1/2 cup light brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon black peppercorns
- 1 1/2 teaspoons allspice berries
- 1 1/2 teaspoons chopped candied ginger
- 1 gallon ice water
- 1 red apple, quartered
- 1/2 yellow onion, quartered
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 1 cup water
- 4 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 6 fresh sage leaves
- Canola oil

### Directions
1. In a large pot, bring the broth, salt, brown sugar, peppercorns, allspice, and ginger to a boil, stirring to dissolve the salt and sugar. Cool completely, then chill.
2. Combine the cold brine with the ice water in a food-safe container large enough to hold the turkey. Submerge the turkey and keep it refrigerated or at 40°F or colder for 8 to 16 hours, turning it once halfway through.
3. Remove the turkey from the brine, discard the brine, and pat the turkey dry. Heat the oven to 500°F.
4. Put the apple, onion, cinnamon stick, and water in a microwave-safe dish. Microwave for 5 minutes, then place the solids in the turkey cavity with the rosemary and sage. Tuck the wings under the bird and lightly coat the skin with oil.
5. Roast on a rack in a roasting pan for 30 minutes. Reduce the oven to 350°F and continue roasting until a thermometer in the thickest part of the breast and innermost thigh reads 165°F, about 1 1/2 to 2 hours more. If the skin browns too quickly, cover it loosely with foil.
6. Rest the turkey for at least 15 minutes before carving.

## Creamy Mashed Potatoes

### Ingredients
- 2 pounds Yukon gold potatoes
- 1/2 cup heavy cream
- 4 ounces unsalted butter, cut into pieces
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper

### Directions
1. Peel the potatoes and cut them into even 1/2-inch pieces. Put them in a saucepan, cover with cold water by at least an inch, and bring to a boil.
2. Uncover, lower the heat to a simmer, and cook for 15 to 20 minutes, until the potatoes are very tender. Drain.
3. In the empty saucepan, warm the cream, butter, salt, and pepper until the butter melts. Remove from the heat.
4. Pass the potatoes through a food mill into the cream mixture, working in batches, then stir gently to combine. Taste and adjust the seasoning.

## Sweet Corn Bread Pudding

### Ingredients
- 1/2 onion, finely diced
- 1 ounce unsalted butter
- 1/2 teaspoon thyme
- 1/2 teaspoon rosemary
- 1 (15-ounce) can creamed-style sweet corn
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 2 eggs
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1/2 cup stone-ground yellow cornmeal
- 1/2 cup shredded Parmesan
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- Ground black pepper, to taste
- 2 cups French bread, cubed

### Directions
1. Heat the oven to 350°F. In an oven-safe skillet, cook the onion in the butter with the thyme and rosemary until softened.
2. In a bowl, mix the corn, cream, eggs, baking powder, cornmeal, Parmesan, salt, and pepper. Fold in the bread cubes.
3. Spread the mixture over the onions in the skillet. Bake for about 50 minutes, until set. Let cool slightly before serving.

## Tart Cranberry Dipping Sauce

### Ingredients
- 1 pound frozen cranberries
- 2 cups orange juice
- 3 cups ginger ale
- 2 tablespoons maple syrup
- 2 tablespoons light brown sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
- Zest of 1 orange

### Directions
1. Combine all the ingredients in a nonreactive saucepan and bring to a boil.
2. Lower the heat and simmer, stirring occasionally, for 30 to 45 minutes, until the liquid has reduced by about half.
3. Carefully blend until smooth. Taste and adjust the seasoning, then serve in small bowls.
