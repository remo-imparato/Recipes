# Bread Stuffing

## Ingredients
- 1 loaf bread, cubed
- 1 stick butter
- 1 onion, diced
- 3 celery stalks, diced
- 1 tablespoon sage
- 1 tablespoon thyme
- 1 1/2 cups broth
- Salt and pepper, to taste

## Directions
1. Heat the oven to 350°F and grease a baking dish.
2. Melt the butter in a skillet over medium heat. Add the onion and celery and cook until softened.
3. Combine the bread cubes, cooked vegetables, sage, thyme, salt, and pepper in a large bowl. Gradually stir in the broth until the bread is moistened.
4. Transfer the mixture to the baking dish and bake for 30 to 35 minutes, until heated through and golden on top.
