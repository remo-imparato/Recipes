# Roasted Sweet Potatoes

## Ingredients
- 4 medium sweet potatoes, peeled and cut into cubes
- 3 tablespoons butter, melted
- 3 tablespoons brown sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon
- Pinch of salt

## Directions
1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
2. Toss the sweet potatoes with the melted butter, brown sugar, cinnamon, and salt.
3. Spread the potatoes in a single layer on a baking sheet. Roast for 25 to 30 minutes, turning once, until tender and browned.
