# Steamed Artichokes

## Ingredients
- Artichokes (quantity not specified)
- 1 garlic clove
- 1/2 onion
- A little cider vinegar
- Celery (quantity not specified)
- Salt
- Butter
- Lemon juice
- Water, for steaming

## Directions
1. Trim off the outer leaves. Place the artichokes in a steamer inside a pot with a few inches of water. The source's sentence about what else to put in the pot is incomplete; it lists garlic, onion, vinegar, and celery but does not explain their use.
2. Cover and steam for about 30 minutes, or until an outer leaf can be plucked off easily.
3. Melt butter, add lemon juice, and dip the leaves in the lemon butter. Pull the edible part from the leaves with your teeth, as described in the source.
