# Broccoli-Rice Casserole

## Ingredients
- 1 square package frozen chopped broccoli
- 1 can cream of mushroom soup
- 1 jar Cheese Whiz
- 1/2 diced onion
- 1/2 stick real butter
- Minced garlic
- 1 1/2 cups uncooked Minute Rice

## Directions
1. Cook the broccoli according to the package directions.
2. Melt the Cheese Whiz according to the jar directions.
3. Saute the onion and garlic in a pan with the butter.
4. Mix all the ingredients in a bowl and place the mixture in a casserole dish.
5. Bake uncovered at 350 F for 35 minutes, or until the rice is soft.
