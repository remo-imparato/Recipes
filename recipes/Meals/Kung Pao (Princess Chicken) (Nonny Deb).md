# Kung Pao (Princess Chicken) (Nonny Deb)

## Ingredients
- 2 lb chicken thighs, or 1 lb chicken breasts
- 2 Tbsp cornstarch
- 1 Tbsp soy sauce
- 10 dried hot red peppers, about 1 inch long
- 1 Tbsp rice wine vinegar
- 2 Tbsp soy sauce
- 1 Tbsp sugar
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp cornstarch
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 cup vegetable oil, for frying
- 1 tsp minced fresh ginger root
- 1/2 cup chopped roasted peanuts

## Directions
1. Remove bones from the chicken, lightly pound the meat, and cut into 1-inch pieces. Combine 2 Tbsp cornstarch and 1 Tbsp soy sauce in a medium bowl. Add chicken, mix well, and let stand for 20 minutes.
2. Trim the stems from the dried peppers. In a small bowl, mix the vinegar, 2 Tbsp soy sauce, sugar, salt, 1 tsp cornstarch, and sesame oil for the sauce.
3. Heat the vegetable oil in a wok. Stir-fry the chicken for about 2 minutes, until very lightly browned. Remove with a slotted spoon and drain over the wok. Remove all but 2 Tbsp oil from the wok.
4. Heat the remaining oil over medium heat. Stir-fry the peppers until dark brown. Add the ginger and chicken and stir-fry for 1 minute.
5. Add the sauce and cook, stirring, until it thickens slightly. Stir in the peanuts.
