# Good Eats Roast Turkey (Alton Brown)

## Ingredients
- 1 (14- to 16-pound) young turkey, thawed, giblets removed
- 1 gallon vegetable broth
- 1 cup kosher salt
- 1/2 cup light brown sugar
- 1 tablespoon black peppercorns
- 1 1/2 teaspoons allspice berries
- 1 1/2 teaspoons chopped candied ginger
- 1 gallon ice water
- 1 red apple, quartered
- 1/2 yellow onion, quartered
- 1 cinnamon stick
- 1 cup water
- 4 sprigs fresh rosemary
- 6 fresh sage leaves
- Canola oil

## Directions
1. In a large pot, bring the broth, salt, brown sugar, peppercorns, allspice, and ginger to a boil, stirring to dissolve the salt and sugar. Cool completely, then chill.
2. Combine the cold brine with the ice water in a food-safe container large enough to hold the turkey. Submerge the turkey and keep it refrigerated or at 40°F or colder for 8 to 16 hours, turning it once halfway through.
3. Remove the turkey from the brine, discard the brine, and pat the turkey dry. Heat the oven to 500°F.
4. Put the apple, onion, cinnamon stick, and water in a microwave-safe dish. Microwave for 5 minutes, then place the solids in the turkey cavity with the rosemary and sage. Tuck the wings under the bird and lightly coat the skin with oil.
5. Roast on a rack in a roasting pan for 30 minutes. Reduce the oven to 350°F and continue roasting until a thermometer in the thickest part of the breast and innermost thigh reads 165°F, about 1 1/2 to 2 hours more. If the skin browns too quickly, cover it loosely with foil.
6. Rest the turkey for at least 15 minutes before carving.
