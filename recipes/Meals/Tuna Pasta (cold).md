# Tuna Pasta (cold)

## Ingredients
- 1 can solid white tuna in water
- 1 medium scoop real mayonnaise
- 1 1/2 cups uncooked pasta
- 1/2 onion, diced (optional)
- 1 stalk celery, diced (optional)

## Directions
1. Cook the pasta until soft, drain, and rinse in cold water.
2. Drain the tuna, break it up in a large bowl, and add the pasta and mayonnaise.
3. Add the onion and celery, if desired. Stir everything together, cover, and refrigerate for 1 hour.
