# Tuna Pasta (cold)

## Ingredients
- Pasta
- Real mayonnaise
- Tuna
- Salt and pepper

### Optional
- Diced onions
- Diced celery
- Sliced green olives

## Directions
1. Boil and rinse the pasta. Combine it with the mayonnaise and tuna, adding any optional ingredients; season with salt and pepper.
2. Refrigerate for 1 hour to chill.
