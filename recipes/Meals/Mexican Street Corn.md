# Mexican Street Corn

Serves 4

## Ingredients
- 2 tablespoons vegetable oil
- 4 ears corn, shucked, kernels removed (about 3 cups)
- Kosher salt, to taste
- 2 tablespoons mayonnaise
- 2 ounces Cotija or feta cheese, finely crumbled, or Pecorino Romano, grated
- 1/2 cup finely sliced scallion greens
- 1/2 cup fresh cilantro leaves, finely chopped
- 1 jalapeño pepper, seeded and finely chopped
- 1 to 2 medium cloves garlic, minced or grated (1 to 2 teaspoons)
- 1 tablespoon lime juice (from 1 lime)
- Chili powder or red pepper flakes, to taste

## Directions
1. Heat the oil in a large wok or nonstick skillet over high heat until shimmering. Add the corn kernels, season with salt, and toss once or twice. Cook without stirring until charred on the first side, about 2 minutes. Toss and stir the corn, then cook until charred on a second side, about 2 minutes longer. Continue tossing and charring until well charred all over, about 10 minutes total. Transfer to a large bowl.
2. While the corn is still hot, add the mayonnaise, cheese, scallions, cilantro, jalapeño, garlic, lime juice, and chili powder or red pepper flakes. Toss to combine. Taste and adjust the salt and chili seasoning. Serve hot or at room temperature.
