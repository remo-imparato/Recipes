# Ethiopian Chicken Stew

## Ingredients
- 1 (2.5-3 lb) chicken
- 1/2 cup plus 2 Tbsp lemon juice, divided
- 3 cups plus 2 Tbsp water, divided
- 3 Tbsp oil
- 1 large onion, peeled and finely chopped
- 1 1/2 tsp paprika
- 1/4 tsp cayenne pepper
- 1/2 tsp ground ginger
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 Tbsp flour 
- 3 cups cooked white rice

## Directions
1. Combine 1/4 cup lemon juice with 2 cups water. Soak the chicken in the lemon water for 15 minutes, then drain.
2. Heat the oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until golden brown, about 15 minutes.
3. Stir in the paprika, cayenne, ginger, black pepper, and salt. Add 1 cup water and bring to a boil. Add the chicken, cover, reduce the heat, and simmer for 40 minutes or until cooked through, stirring occasionally.
4. Remove the chicken from the cooking liquid and let it cool slightly. Remove the skin and meat from the bones, then shred the meat.
5. The source says to combine the flour with 2 Tbsp lemon juice and "2T flour" again, then add the mixture to the boiling cooking liquid and cook for 3-4 minutes, until thickened. The repeated flour may be a typo; see note.
6. Return the chicken to the sauce and let it sit for a few minutes. Serve with rice.

## Note
The ingredient list calls for 1/2 cup plus 2 Tbsp lemon juice, but the directions use only 1/4 cup plus 2 Tbsp. The directions also repeat flour where a liquid may have been intended in the slurry; confirm before making.
