# Risotto (Nonny Deb)

## Ingredients
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- 1 garlic clove
- 1/2 lb mushrooms, chopped
- 1/2 cup minced parsley
- Salt and pepper
- 1/4 cup butter
- 1 1/2 cups Arborio rice
- 4 cups hot chicken broth, plus more if needed
- Grated Parmesan to taste

## Directions
1. Heat the oil and garlic. Add the mushrooms and parsley and cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, for 5 minutes.
2. Heat the butter in a 2-quart casserole. Add the rice and cook for 2 minutes, until opaque.
3. Add the hot broth a little at a time, stirring constantly and allowing it to be absorbed before adding more. More broth may be needed.
4. Combine with the mushroom mixture and cheese.
