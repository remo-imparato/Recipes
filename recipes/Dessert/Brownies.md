# Brownies

## Ingredients
- 4 eggs
- 2 cups sugar
- 1 cup flour
- 1 tsp vanilla
- 4 oz unsweetened chocolate
- 2 sticks butter

## Directions
1. Melt the chocolate and butter in a double boiler. Let cool so the mixture does not cook the eggs.
2. Mix the eggs and sugar well, then stir in the flour.
3. Stir in the chocolate-butter mixture.
4. Bake at 350 F for 30 minutes.
