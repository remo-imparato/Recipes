# Pumpkin Pie (Nonny Deb)

## Ingredients
- 2 eggs (listed twice in the source; unclear whether 2 eggs total or 4)
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/4 cup maple syrup
- 1 1/2 cups sour cream
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 1/2 tsp ginger
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- 1/4 tsp cloves
- 2 cups pumpkin
- Pie crust (store-bought or use another recipe)

## Directions
1. The source does not give mixing or filling instructions. It says to buy a pie crust or consult Grandma Google.
2. Bake for 15 minutes at 425 F, then 45 minutes at 350 F.
