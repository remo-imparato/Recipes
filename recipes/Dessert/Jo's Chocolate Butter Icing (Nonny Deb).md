# Jo's Chocolate Butter Icing (Nonny Deb)

## Ingredients
- 3 oz unsweetened chocolate (see substitution below)
- 3 Tbsp butter
- 1/4 cup milk
- About 2 cups powdered sugar
- 1 tsp vanilla
- Salt (mentioned in the directions, but no amount is given)

## Directions
1. Melt the chocolate and butter. Remove from the heat.
2. Add the milk and salt.
3. Gradually add the powdered sugar and vanilla.

## Notes
- For each ounce of unsweetened chocolate, substitute 3 Tbsp cocoa and 1 Tbsp butter. For this recipe, use 9 Tbsp cocoa and 3 Tbsp additional butter.
- The transcription reads "14 c" for the milk; the revised cookbook lists 1/4 cup.
