# Apple Crisp (Nonny Deb)

## Ingredients
- 4 cups apples, peeled and quartered

### Topping
- 1/4 cup water
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 6 Tbsp butter

## Directions
1. Arrange the apples in a baking dish.
2. Add the water, sugar, flour, cinnamon, and butter as the topping.
3. Bake at 350 F for 45 minutes.
