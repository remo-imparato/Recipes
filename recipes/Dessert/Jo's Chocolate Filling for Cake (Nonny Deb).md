# Jo's Chocolate Filling for Cake (Nonny Deb)

## Ingredients
- 4 squares bitter chocolate
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 cups milk, plus 1/2 cup additional milk for pudding consistency
- 2 tsp butter
- 4 heaping tsp butter (listed separately in the source)

## Directions
1. Cook the chocolate, sugar, milk, and butter in a double boiler, stirring until thick.
2. For pudding, add 1/2 cup more milk, as noted in the source.

## Notes
- The source says this filling was doubled per Remo's suggestion on 2/7/2024.
- It lists both 2 tsp butter and 4 heaping tsp butter; it is unclear whether these are separate amounts or a correction, so both are recorded.
- Intended for the Clark family's traditional birthday cake made with yellow cake; no yellow-cake recipe is provided.
