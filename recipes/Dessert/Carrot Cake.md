# Carrot Cake

## Ingredients
- 1 1/2 cups oil
- 2 cups sugar
- 4 eggs
- 2 cups flour
- 2 tsp baking soda
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 2 tsp cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp nutmeg
- 1 cup pecans or walnuts
- 3 cups (1 lb) grated carrots
- 1 cup golden raisins
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Directions
1. Blend the oil and sugar. Add the eggs one at a time.
2. Sift the dry ingredients together, add them, and blend well.
3. Pour into greased and floured pans: three cake pans or one 9-by-11-inch baking dish.
4. Bake at 350 F for 1 hour.
