# Jo's Chocolate Filling for Cake (Nonny Deb)

## Ingredients
- 4 squares bitter chocolate
- 1 cup sugar
- 2 cups milk
- 2 tsp butter
- 4 heaping tsp butter

## Directions
1. Cook the filling in a double boiler, stirring until thick.

## Pudding Variation
Add 1/2 cup more milk.

## Notes
- The source calls this "Great Grandma Jo's Chocolate Filling for Cake." The amounts shown are already doubled per Remo's suggestion on February 7, 2024.
- The source lists both butter amounts without saying whether the second replaces the first.
- Intended for the Clark family's traditional birthday cake made with yellow cake; no yellow-cake recipe is provided.
