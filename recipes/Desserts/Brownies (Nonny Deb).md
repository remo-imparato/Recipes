# Brownies (Nonny Deb)

## Ingredients
- 4 squares unsweetened chocolate
- 2 sticks butter
- 4 eggs
- 2 cups sugar (the source says a little less may be used)
- 1 cup flour
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Directions
1. Melt the chocolate and butter.
2. Mix the eggs, sugar, flour, and vanilla. Combine with the melted chocolate and butter.
3. Grease an 8 1/2-by-11-inch Pyrex pan. Bake at 350 F for 30 minutes.
