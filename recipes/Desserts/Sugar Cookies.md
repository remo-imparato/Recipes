# Sugar Cookies

## Ingredients
- 1 cup butter
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1 egg
- 2 1/2 tsp vanilla
- 3 1/4 cups flour
- 1 1/2 tsp baking powder

## Directions
1. Cream the butter and sugar together. Add the egg and vanilla.
2. Add the flour and baking powder. Work with half of the dough and refrigerate the rest.
3. Roll the dough out thinly 1/4 inch and bake at 425 F for 8-10 minutes.
