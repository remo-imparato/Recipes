# Peaches with Panna Cotta (Nonny Deb)

## Ingredients
### Peaches
- 3 peaches, thinly sliced
- 1 Tbsp lemon juice
- 1 Tbsp honey

### Panna Cotta
- 1 1/4 tsp unflavored gelatin
- 2 Tbsp water
- 1 1/4 cups cream
- 1 cup yogurt
- 1/4 cup honey
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Directions
1. Toss the peaches with 1 Tbsp lemon juice and 1 Tbsp honey. Let stand at room temperature for 20 minutes.
2. Soften the gelatin in the water for 1 minute. Stir in the cream and heat over medium heat, stirring, until the gelatin dissolves.
3. Whisk together the 1/4 cup honey, yogurt, and vanilla. The source says to add this mixture to the peaches.
4. Pour into bowls and chill for 8 hours.

## Note
The source does not clearly say when to combine the gelatin-cream mixture with the peach-yogurt mixture; no additional steps are inferred.
