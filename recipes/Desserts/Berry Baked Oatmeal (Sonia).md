# Berry Baked Oatmeal (Sonia)

## Ingredients
- 2 cups rolled oats
- 1 teaspoon baking powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
- ¼ teaspoon salt
- 1¾ cups milk (dairy or non-dairy)
- 1 large egg
- 2 tablespoons maple syrup
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
- 1½ cups mixed berries (fresh or frozen)
- Extra berries for topping (optional)

## Directions
1. Preheat the oven to 375°F (190°C) and lightly grease an 8x8-inch baking dish.
2. In a large bowl, combine the rolled oats, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt.
3. In a separate bowl, whisk together the milk, egg, maple syrup, and vanilla extract.
4. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir until well combined.
5. Fold in the mixed berries.
6. Transfer the mixture to the prepared baking dish and spread evenly.
7. Top with additional berries if desired.
8. Bake for 35–40 minutes, or until the oatmeal is set and lightly golden on top.
9. Let cool for 5–10 minutes before serving.
10. Serve warm as is, or with extra milk, yogurt, or maple syrup.
