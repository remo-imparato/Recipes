# Spicy Tuna Shooters

## Ingredients
- 2 oz fresh sushi-grade tuna, finely diced
- 1 Tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sriracha, or to taste
- 1 tsp sesame oil
- 1 tsp rice vinegar
- 1/2 tsp wasabi paste
- 1 Tbsp green onions, finely chopped
- 1 tsp tobiko (fish roe), for garnish
- 1/2 tsp sesame seeds
- 1 oz vodka or sake (optional)
- 1 slice cucumber or 1 lime wedge, for garnish

## Directions
1. In a small bowl, mix the tuna with the soy sauce, sriracha, sesame oil, rice vinegar, and wasabi paste.
2. Spoon the mixture into a shot glass.
3. Sprinkle with the green onions, tobiko, and sesame seeds. If using, pour a small amount of vodka or sake over the tuna.
4. Garnish with cucumber or lime and serve immediately.

## Ra-yu (Chili Sesame Oil)
### Ingredients
- 1 Tbsp rapeseed or vegetable oil
- 1 Tbsp sesame oil
- 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 1 tsp fresh ginger, finely chopped
- 1 spring onion, white part finely chopped
- Chopped chili pepper

## Variations
- **Crispy rice:** Serve the spicy tuna mixture on crispy sushi rice and garnish with jalapeno slices.
- **Tropical:** Add diced mango or pineapple. Use coconut-infused sake instead of vodka.
- **Korean-inspired:** Mix in gochujang and top with kimchi.
- **Cucumber cups:** Hollow out cucumber slices and use them as edible cups.
- **Avocado:** Layer the tuna mixture over mashed avocado and sprinkle with crushed wasabi peas.
- **Yuzu citrus:** Replace the rice vinegar with yuzu juice and garnish with shiso leaves.
