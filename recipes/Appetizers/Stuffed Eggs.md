# Stuffed Eggs

## Ingredients
- 12 eggs
- 4 Tbsp mayonnaise
- 2 Tbsp sour cream
- 6 tsp herbs (chives)
- 1 tsp mustard powder
- 1 tsp prepared horseradish
- 3 tsp vinegar
- 3 tsp shallots or green onion
- 1/2 tsp curry powder
- A few drops hot pepper sauce
- Paprika, for sprinkling

## Variation: Deviled Eggs

### Ingredients
- 12 eggs
- 1/2 cup mayonnaise
- 1 teaspoon mustard
- 1 teaspoon vinegar
- Salt and pepper, to taste
- Paprika, for garnish

### Directions
1. Add about 1 inch of water to a pot and bring it to a boil. Place the cold, refrigerated eggs in a steamer basket. For centered yolks, store the eggs on their sides before cooking.
2. Steam the eggs for 13 minutes.
3. Immediately transfer the eggs to an ice bath and chill them for the same amount of time as they steamed.
4. Peel the eggs under running water, halve them lengthwise, and remove the yolks.
5. Mash the yolks with the mayonnaise, mustard, vinegar, salt, and pepper.
6. Spoon or pipe the filling into the egg whites and garnish with paprika.
