# Crab Loaf

## Ingredients
- 1 lb crabmeat (backfin and lump)
- 3 Tbsp self-rising flour or pancake mix
- 4 shakes Worcestershire sauce
- 1 egg
- 1 Tbsp parsley
- 1 Tbsp prepared mustard
- 2 Tbsp mayonnaise (source note: "generones"; unclear)
- 3/4 cup oil, for frying

## Directions
1. Put the crabmeat in a bowl and pick out any shell, taking care not to break up the lumps.
2. Add all the ingredients except the oil and blend gently with a two-pronged fork.
3. Heat the oil. Form cakes with an ice-cream scoop and fry until golden brown on one side, then turn.
4. Drain on paper towels.
