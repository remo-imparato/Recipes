Roast Chicken

1 whole free-range chicken, from 3 1/4 to 3 1 2/ pounds, no larger
 
1 1/2 teaspoons salt, or less if desired
freshly ground black pepper
2 or 3 sprigs fresh thyme
2 or 3 small shallots, peeled (divided use)
lemon or lime peel, 3- or 4-inch-size pieces
1 cup water 
1 tablespoon butter
1 tablespoon oil, such as grapeseed
1 large bay leaf, broken into pieces
2 cloves garlic, coarsely chopped
1 teaspoon finely chopped thyme
1 cup water or chicken broth
 

Preheat the oven to 475 degrees. Rinse chicken well with water, using paper towel to clean out the cavity. Remove any feather pins.
Pat the chicken dry with paper towels. Rub chicken all over with salt and black pepper, including the cavity. Place the thyme sprigs, 2 shallots and lemon or lime peel into the cavity of the chicken.
Using twine or heavy string, tie the chicken legs close to body and fold the wing tips back and secure with skewers or twine. Lightly oil a shallow roasting pan (a 13-inch-by-9-inch is best, no larger) and place the chicken breast side up in the pan.
1. Pour water (or mixture) into the roasting pan around the chicken, along with the butter, oil, bay leaf, garlic, thyme and a coarsely chopped shallot. Set pan on lower oven rack and bake 15 minutes. 
2-Using heavy gloves and a wooden spoon or spatula, turn the chicken over, breast side down. Roast 15 minutes. 
3-Set chicken breast side up again. Roast chicken for 15 minutes longer. 
4-Lower the oven temperature to 425 degrees.Turn chicken on breast again & bake another 15 minutes. 
5-Turn chicken over & roast for 10-15 minutes longer, or until juices run clear when pierced with a knife at the thigh and at the wing/breast juncture. An instant read thermometer should read about 175 degrees.
When done, move the chicken to a warm platter and let rest about 10 minutes in a warm place, sitting up on its haunches or breast to allow the juices to drain out. Don't tent the bird with foil; that causes steam to collect. The back of the stove top is a good spot to stand the chicken up while it drains.

Just before serving, skim off the excess oil in the roasting pan with a spoon , leaving a tablespoon or so, if desired. Stir in the wine and water or chicken broth. Place the pan over high heat and bring to a boil, scrapping up the browned bits in the pan.

Heat for 4 or 5 minutes, stirring. Strain the gravy in a warm bowl and serve with the chicken.

Remove bones from chicken. Lightly pound meat with whatever and chop into 1” pieces. Combine cornstarch, and soy sauce in a medium bowl. Add chicken and mix well. Let stand 20 min. Trim stems from the dried peppers. combine sauce ingredients in small bowl, mix well, set aside
Heat oil in a wok 1 min. Stir fry chicken pieces about 2 min unti very lightly browned. Remove with a slotted spoon, drain well over wok. Remove oil from wok except 2 T. Hear that oil over medium heat, stir fry the peppers until dark brown. Add ginger and chicken. Stir-fry 1 min. Add sauce, stir well, cook until sauce thickens slightly. stir in chopped peanuts.

Chocolate Chip and Orange muffins
 
3 c. all purpose flour
1 ¼ c sugar
2 t baking powder
1 ¼ c milk
¾ c vegetable oil
2 large eggs
4 t grated orange peel
12 oz  (1.5 c. ) chocolate
 

Preheat oven to 350. Line a/2 c muffin cups with foil liners. Combine flour, sugar and baking poweder in large bowl. Make well in center of mixture. In another bowl, mix milk, oil, eggs and orange peel. Stir milk mixture into dry ingredients (mixture will be lumpy) Add chocolate chips to batter. Divide batter among muffin cups. Bake until tester comes out clean, about 20 min.
