Chocolate Chip and Orange Muffins

Ingredients
	3 cups all-purpose flour
	1 1/4 cups sugar
	2 tsp baking powder
	1 1/4 cups milk
	3/4 cup vegetable oil
	2 large eggs
	4 tsp grated orange peel
	12 oz chocolate (the source also says 1 1/2 cups)

Directions
1. Preheat the oven to 350 F. Line muffin cups with foil liners.
2. Combine the flour, sugar, and baking powder in a large bowl. Make a well in the center.
3. In another bowl, mix the milk, oil, eggs, and orange peel. Stir this into the dry ingredients; the batter will be lumpy.
4. Stir in the chocolate and divide the batter among the muffin cups.
5. Bake for about 20 minutes, until a tester comes out clean.

Note
The source gives chocolate as both 12 oz and 1 1/2 cups; both are retained.
