Cake
	1/4 c butter 
	6 eggs, beaten
	1 c sugar
	1 tsp vanilla
	1 c sifted cake flour
Preheat oven to 350. melt butter and put aside.  In a double boiler, heat the eggs to lukewarm. Add 2/3 c sugar and beat for 7 min at medium speed.  Add 1/3 c sugar and increase speed and beat 2 more minutes until it looks like a continuous, flat ribbon when dropped from a spoon.  Add the flour, then the melted butter.  Pour batter into greased and floured cake pans and bake 30-40 min


Chocolate Filling (1/2 recipe :)
	2 oz. bittersweet chocolate
	1/2 c sugar
	1 c milk
	1 tsp butter 
	2 Tbs cornstarch (heaping spoonful)
Melt the chocolate and butter in a double boiler. Do all this over the heat: Add the sugar.  Use some of the milk to make a cornstarch slurry, add this and the rest of the milk to the chocolate; stir over heat until thick (5-10 min). Let cool to thicken. Split each cake layer in half, divide the chocolate filling in 3rds. Spread between each split and between the cake.

Chocolate Frosting (3/4 recipe :)
	3 oz. bittersweet chocolate
	3 Tbs butter
	1/4 c milk
	2 c powdered sugar
	1 tsp vanilla
	1/8 tsp salt
Melt chocolate and butter in double boiler.  Remove from heat and use electric beaters to add milk and salt slowly.  Add the sugar until the frosting stands in thick peaks.
