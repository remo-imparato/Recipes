# Apple Crisp

## Ingredients
- 4-8 apples, cored, peeled, and sliced
- 1/2 cup sugar
- 1/2 tsp cinnamon
- Juice of 1/2 lemon
- 1 cup flour
- 1 cup brown sugar
- 1/2 cup butter, softened
- 1/3 cup chopped pecans (optional topping addition)

## Directions
1. Mix the apples with the sugar, cinnamon, and lemon juice.
2. Arrange the apples in a buttered 8-inch pan to a depth of about 1 1/2 inches.
3. Mix the flour, brown sugar, and butter; spread the mixture over the apples. Add the pecans to the topping if desired.
4. Bake at 350 F for 30 minutes. Serve with hard sauce.
