# Apple Crisp (Deb)

## Ingredients
- 4 cups apples, peeled and quartered
- 1/4 cup water
- 3/4 cup sugar
- 1/2 cup flour
- 1 tsp cinnamon
- 6 Tbsp butter

## Directions
1. Assemble the ingredients in a baking dish.
2. Bake at 350 F for 45 minutes.

## Note
The source labels the water, sugar, flour, cinnamon, and butter as ?Topping? but does not explain how to combine them or layer them.
