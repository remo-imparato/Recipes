Sugar Cookies

	1 c butter
	1/2 c sugar
	1 egg
	2 1/2 tsp vanilla
	3 1/4 c flour
	1 1/2 tsp baking powder
	
Mix butter and sugar together using creaming method.  Add the egg and vanilla.  Add the flour and baking powder.  Work with half and refrigerate the rest.  Roll out thin.  Bake 425 for 10 min.
