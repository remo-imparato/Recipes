Apple Crisp

	4-8 apples, cored, peeled and sliced
	1/2 c sugar
	1/2 tsp cinnamon
	Juice of 1/2 lemon
	1 c flour
	1 c brown sugar
	1/2 c butter, softened
	Add 1/3 c. chopped pecans to the topping
Mix apples with sugar, cinnamon and lemon juice. Lay apples in buttered 8" pan to depth of 1.5". Mix flour brown sugar and butter, spread on top. Bake at 350 for 30 min.  Serve with hard sauce
