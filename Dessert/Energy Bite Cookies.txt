Energy Bites Cookies
1/2 cup peanut butter
1/3 cup honey
1 tsp vanilla extract
1 cup old fashioned oats
2/3 cup toasted, sweetened shredded coconut
1/2 cup golden flaxseed
6 Tbsp chocolate chips

Mix honey peanut butter vanilla, add the rest
Chill for 30 min in fridge until firm
Form into balls and store in refrigerator in air tight container
