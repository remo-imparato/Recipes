Chocolate Chip Cookies

	1 lb chocolate chips
	1 c butter (room temp)
	1 c brown sugar (packed)
	2/3 c white sugar
	2 1/2 c flour (sifted)
	2 eggs
	1tsp salt
	1tsp baking soda
	2 Tbl hot water
	1 tsp vanilla
	
Mix the butter, sugar and vanilla together using the creaming method. Dissolve the baking soda in the hot water, add with eggs. Mix in flour and salt.  Cook at 350 for 10-12 min
