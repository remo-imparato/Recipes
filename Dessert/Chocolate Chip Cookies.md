# Chocolate Chip Cookies

## Ingredients
- 1 lb chocolate chips
- 1 cup butter, at room temperature
- 1 cup packed brown sugar
- 2/3 cup white sugar
- 2 1/2 cups sifted flour
- 2 eggs
- 1 tsp salt
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 2 Tbsp hot water
- 1 tsp vanilla

## Directions
1. Cream the butter, sugars, and vanilla together.
2. Dissolve the baking soda in the hot water and add it with the eggs.
3. Mix in the flour and salt, then stir in the chocolate chips.
4. Bake at 350 F for 10-12 minutes.
