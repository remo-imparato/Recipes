Chili

1 lb beef, mixed raw with 1/2 cup beef broth. Heat on high stirring until broth evaporates then brown the meat (this breaks the meat up into small pieces)
1 diced onion and cook on low with the meat until translucent

1tsp anchovy paste (unami)
1 can chili beans
1 can red kidney beans
1Tbs chili powder to taste
Salt pepper to taste
Cover, simmer, at more broth as necessary
