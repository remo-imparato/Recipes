# Pot Roast (Deb)

## Ingredients
- 3-4 lb chuck roast
- Garlic (quantity not specified)
- Flour, for dredging
- 2 Tbsp oil
- 1 carrot
- 1 celery stalk, diced
- 1 small onion, studded with 3 cloves
- 2 cups boiling stock
- 1 bay leaf
- About 6 small potatoes, such as Yukon Gold

## Directions
1. Rub the meat with garlic. Dredge it in flour and brown it in oil on all sides.
2. When the meat is halfway browned, add the vegetables and stock.
3. Cover and cook for 3-4 hours at 325 F, either in the oven or by simmering on the stove. Add more liquid if necessary.
