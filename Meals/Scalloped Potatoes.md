# Scalloped Potatoes

## Ingredients
- 4-5 potatoes, thinly sliced
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 Tbsp flour
- 3-6 Tbsp butter
- 1/4 cup saut?ed onions
- 1/4 cup thinly sliced peppers
- 1/4 tsp dry mustard
- Ham or bacon (quantity not specified)
- 1 1/4 cups milk
- 1/4 tsp paprika

## Directions
1. Parboil the potatoes for 8-10 minutes, then drain.
2. Arrange the potatoes in three layers in a greased 10-inch baking dish. Sprinkle each layer with flour and dot with butter. Add the onions and chopped ham or bacon.
3. Heat the milk and season it with paprika and dry mustard. Pour over the potatoes.
4. Bake at 350 F for 25 minutes.
