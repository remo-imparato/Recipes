Smoked Brisket

Brine: 1c sugar, 1c salt, boil in 2c water, mix in 1 lb ice, once cool,  soak over night add whole pepper corn, garlic powder

225 degrees Smoke for 4-8 hours(1.5 hours per lb) fat side up, until 165 internal temp. Wrap in foil and cook until 185-190 internal temp - about 2 more hours. Then rest with the fatty side up, wrapped in layers of foil in a warm cooler for 2-4 hours.
