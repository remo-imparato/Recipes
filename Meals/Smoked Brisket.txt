Smoked Brisket

Ingredients
Brine
	1 cup sugar
	1 cup salt
	2 cups water
	1 lb ice
	Whole peppercorns
	Garlic powder

Brisket
	Brisket (the source does not specify a weight)

Directions
1. Bring the sugar, salt, and water to a boil. Mix in the ice and let the brine cool. Add the brisket, whole peppercorns, and garlic powder; soak overnight.
2. Smoke at 225 F for 4-8 hours (about 1.5 hours per pound), fat side up, until the internal temperature reaches 165 F.
3. Wrap in foil and cook until the internal temperature reaches 185-190 F, about 2 more hours.
4. Rest fat side up, wrapped in layers of foil in a warm cooler, for 2-4 hours.

Note
The source does not specify the quantities of peppercorns or garlic powder, or the brisket weight.
