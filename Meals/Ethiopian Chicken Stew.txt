Ethiopian Chicken Stew

1 (2.5-3 lbs.) cut-up best-of-fryer chicken
1/4 c + 2Tbs lemon juice, divided
3 Tbs oil
1 large onion, peeled and finely chopped
1 1/2 tsp. paprika
1/4 tsp. cayenne pepper
1/2 tsp. ground ginger
1/4 tsp. black pepper
1/2 tsp. salt
1 Tbs flour
3 c cooked white rice

Remove the skin from the chicken.  Combine 1/4 cup lemon juice and 2 cups water. Place the chicken in the lemon-water and let soak 15 minutes. Drain.
Heat the oil in a large pan over medium heat.  Add the onion and cook until golden brown, about 15 minutes.  Stir in the paprika, cayenne, ginger, black pepper and salt.  Add 1 cup water and bring to a boil. Cover, reduce heat and simmer 40 minutes or until cooked through, stirring the chicken occasionally.
Remove the chicken from the cooking liquid and let cool slightly. Remove the chicken meat from the bone and  pull into coarse shreds.
Combine the flour with 2 tablespoons each lemon juice and water. Bring the cooking liquid back to a boil and add the flour mixture.  Cook 3-4 minutes, until thickened. Put the chicken back into he sauce and let sit 2-3 minutes. Serve the chicken and sauce over rice.
