Boston Baked Beans

BOSTON BAKED BEANS
	• 2 LBS White beans (I prefer navy beans, but Great Northern work as well)
	• 1 tsp. baking soda
	• I large onion, peeled
	• I Lb. bacon, chopped up
	• 1/2 cup brown sugar
	• 2/3 cup molasses
	• 2 tsp. dry mustard
	• 4 tsp. salt
	• ½ tsp. pepper

Wash beans and pick out discolored.  Soak beans overnight with water to cover.
Drain, rinse. In a large stovetop pan, cover beans with water, add baking soda, and bring to a boil.  Cook for 10 minutes (watch for boil over).
Drain, rinse, and rinse again.

Place onion in the bottom of a 3 qt casserole.  Put half of the bacon on the bottom, then beans, then the rest of the bacon.  Mix sugar, molasses, mustard, salt and pepper.  Pour over the beans and add water to cover.  Don’t stir.

Bake at 300 degrees, covered, for 6 hours.

After 2-3 hours, stir to see if need more water.  I just look to see if water has gotten well below the top of the beans and add enough to keep moist.  How much you add depends on how dry you like your beans.
