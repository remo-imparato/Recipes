# Pasta with Tuna and Spinach

## Ingredients
- Linguine (quantity not specified)
- Garlic (quantity not specified)
- Onion (quantity not specified)
- 1 lb fresh spinach
- 1 can organic tomatoes (size not specified)
- 1 can tuna (size not specified)
- Oregano (quantity not specified)

## Directions
1. Saut? the garlic, then add the onion and cook until wilted.
2. Add the spinach and cook. Add the tomatoes and tuna and simmer briefly.
3. Add oregano and serve over linguine.
