# Broccoli-Rice Casserole

## Ingredients

- 1 Package Frozen Chopped Broccoli
- 1 Can Cream of Mushroom Soup
- 1 Jar Cheese Whiz
- 1/2 Diced Onion
- 1/2 Stick of Real Butter
- Minced Garlic
- 1 1/2 Cups UNCOOKED Minute Rice

## Directions

1. Cook the broccoli according to the directions on the package.
2. Melt the Cheese Whiz according to the directions on the jar.
3. Sautee the onion & garlic in a pan with the butter.
4. Mix all ingredients in a bowl.
5. Place mixture in a casserole dish.
6. Cook (uncovered) for 35 minutes at 350 degrees.
