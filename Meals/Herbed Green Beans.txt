1 lb. fresh green beans
3-4 qts rappidly boiling water
4 Tbs Butter
1/2 cup minced onion
1/2 clove garlic, minced1/4 cup minced celery
1/2 cup minced parsley
1 tsp fresh rosemary or 1/4 tsp dried
1/4 tsp dried basil
3/4 tsp salt

Trim ends from beans, then cut them diagonally into 2.-inch pieces. Blanch for 6 to 8 min or until tender crisp. Drain and rinse in cold water. Melt butter in skillet. Saute onion, garlic and celery, cookling for about 5 minutes. add parsley, rosemary, basil and salt; cover and simmer fot 10 minutes. before serving, toss and warm the green beans with the herb-falvored butter.
