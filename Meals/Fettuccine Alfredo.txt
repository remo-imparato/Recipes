	• 24 ounces dry fettuccini pasta 
	• 1/2 cup butter
	• 3-4 Tablespoons flour
	• 1 cup heavy cream
	• 1/2 cup milk
	• 1/8 cup Lemon Juice
	• 1/4 teaspoon garlic powder
	• 2 cups mixed grated Parmesan, Parmigiano, and/or Romano
	• Salt
	• White pepper
	• nutmeg

Cook pasta. Melt butter until done bubbling, add flour to make a blonde rue. Add spice and liquids, bring to low simmer, mix in cheese in batches.
