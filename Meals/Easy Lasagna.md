# Easy Lasagna

## Ingredients

- 1 Box Noodles
- 1 lb. Ground Beef
- 1/2 Diced Onion
- Minced Garlic
- 2 Jars of Spaghetti Sauce
- Oregano
- 1 (15 oz.) Container of Ricotta Cheese
- 1 Egg
- 1 Pkg. Frozen Chopped Spinach
- Parmesan Cheese
- 2 Cups Shredded Mozzarella Cheese

## Directions

1. Boil the noodles, rinse and set aside.
2. Brown the ground beef, with onion & garlic. Drain grease.
3. Add the spaghetti sauce with some oregano to the meat.
4. Cook spinach according to the directions on the package.
5. In a separate bowl, combine ricotta cheese, egg, 1 cup of parmesan cheese and 1/2 cup of mozzarella cheese, salt & pepper.
6. Layer ingredients in a casserole dish:
   - Thin layer of sauce on the bottom of the dish
   - Noodles
   - Sauce
   - Ricotta mix
   - Mozzarella cheese
   - Repeat until the pan is full

Cook at 350 degrees for 40-50 minutes, let sit for 15 minutes before cutting or serving.
