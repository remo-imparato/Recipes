1 corned beef brisket (4-5 lbs)
2 cloves garlic
4 bay leaves
whole cloves1/3 cup brown sugar
1/3 cup catsup
3 Tbs vinegar
1 Tbs prepared mustard

Roll and tie beef. place in Dutch oven; cover with cold water. 
add garlic and bay leaves. cover and simmer for 3 hours, or 
until thender. Cool in liquid. Drain. stud fat side wiht cloves. 
blend remaining ingredients; spread on meat. Bake, uncovered, 
at 275 about 1 hour, basting occasionally. 
(or place 6-8 inches above charcoal burned until covered 
with gray ash. Grill, turning and basting occasionally, 
for 30 to 40 minutes)
