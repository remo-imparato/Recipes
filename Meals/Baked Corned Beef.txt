Baked Corned Beef

Ingredients
	1 corned beef brisket (4-5 lb)
	2 cloves garlic
	4 bay leaves
	Whole cloves, for studding
	1/3 cup brown sugar
	1/3 cup catsup
	3 Tbsp vinegar
	1 Tbsp prepared mustard

Directions
1. Roll and tie the beef. Place it in a Dutch oven, cover with cold water, and add the garlic and bay leaves.
2. Cover and simmer for 3 hours, or until tender. Cool in the liquid, then drain.
3. Stud the fat side with whole cloves. Blend the brown sugar, catsup, vinegar, and mustard; spread the mixture over the meat.
4. Bake uncovered at 275 F for about 1 hour, basting occasionally.

Grilling Alternative
Place the meat 6-8 inches above charcoal that has burned until covered with gray ash. Grill, turning and basting occasionally, for 30-40 minutes.
