Crab Cakes (Deb)
----------------
Crab Cakes (Deb)

Ingredients
	1 lb backfin crabmeat
	3 Tbsp self-rising flour
	4 shakes Worcestershire sauce
	1 egg
	1 Tbsp parsley
	1 Tbsp prepared mustard
	2 Tbsp mayonnaise
	3/4 cup oil, for frying

Directions
1. Pick through the crabmeat for shells, taking care not to break up the lumps.
2. Add all ingredients except the oil and blend gently with a two-pronged fork.
3. Heat the oil. Form crab cakes with an ice-cream scoop and fry until golden brown on one side, then turn and fry the other side.
4. Drain on paper towels.

Spicy Tuna Shooters
-------------------
Spicy Tuna Shooters

Ingredients
	2 oz fresh sushi-grade tuna, finely diced
	1 Tbsp soy sauce
	1 tsp sriracha, or to taste
	1 tsp sesame oil
	1 tsp rice vinegar
	1/2 tsp wasabi paste
	1 Tbsp green onions, finely chopped
	1 tsp tobiko (fish roe), for garnish
	1/2 tsp sesame seeds
	1 oz vodka or sake (optional)
	1 slice cucumber or 1 lime wedge, for garnish

Directions
1. In a small bowl, mix the tuna with the soy sauce, sriracha, sesame oil, rice vinegar, and wasabi paste.
2. Spoon the mixture into a shot glass.
3. Sprinkle with the green onions, tobiko, and sesame seeds. If using, pour a small amount of vodka or sake over the tuna.
4. Garnish with cucumber or lime and serve immediately.

Apple Crisp
-----------
Apple Crisp

Ingredients
	4-8 apples, cored, peeled, and sliced
	1/2 cup sugar
	1/2 tsp cinnamon
	Juice of 1/2 lemon
	1 cup flour
	1 cup brown sugar
	1/2 cup butter, softened
	1/3 cup chopped pecans (optional topping addition)

Directions

2. Arrange the apples in a buttered 8-inch pan to a depth of about 1 1/2 inches.

4. Bake at 350 F for 30 minutes. Serve with hard sauce.

Apple Tarte Tatin
-----------------
Apple Tarte Tatin

Ingredients
	4 Tbsp unsalted butter
	2/3 cup sugar
	4 Granny Smith apples
	Juice of 1 lemon
	Pie-crust dough, formed into a 10-inch disk and refrigerated

Directions
1. Preheat the oven to 400 F. Core, peel, and halve the apples. Squeeze the lemon juice over them and set aside.
2. In a 9-inch ovenproof skillet, such as cast iron, melt the butter. Add the sugar and cook over medium heat, whisking constantly, until it resembles melted caramel. Remove from the heat.
3. Cut each apple half into 4 wedges. Arrange the wedges in concentric circles in the pan, cut sides facing down.
4. Put the pie-crust dough on top, tucking in the edges. Bake for 50 minutes.

Pumpkin Pie
-----------
Pumpkin Pie

Ingredients

Pumpkin Puree
	1 (4-6 lb) baking pumpkin, rinsed and dried
	Kosher salt

Crust
	170 g gingersnap cookies
	1 Tbsp dark brown sugar
	1 tsp ground ginger
	2 Tbsp unsalted butter, melted

Filling
	1 (16 oz) can pumpkin puree (or puree prepared as below)
	1 cup half-and-half
	1/2 tsp freshly grated nutmeg
	1/2 tsp kosher salt
	3/4 cup dark brown sugar
	2 large eggs
	1 large egg yolk

Directions

Make the pumpkin puree
1. Heat the oven to 400 F. Line a half-sheet pan with parchment paper.
2. Slice a small piece of skin off one side of the pumpkin so it can lie flat without rolling. Using a large cleaver and mallet, remove the stem and split the pumpkin from top to bottom. Scoop out the seeds and fibers; rinse and reserve the seeds for another use. Cut the fibers with kitchen shears if necessary.
3. Sprinkle the flesh with kosher salt and place the halves flesh-side down on the prepared pan. Roast until a paring knife can be inserted and removed easily, 30-45 minutes; test in several places.
4. Cool the pumpkin on a wire rack for 1 hour. Scoop the flesh from the skin into a food processor and process until smooth, 3-4 minutes. Refrigerate for up to 1 week or freeze for up to 3 months.

Make the crust
1. Heat the oven to 350 F.
2. Process the gingersnaps, brown sugar, and ginger into fine crumbs. Drizzle in the butter and pulse 8-10 times to combine.
3. Press the mixture into the bottom and sides of a 9-inch glass pie dish, just over the lip. Place the dish on a half-sheet pan and bake until lightly browned, 10-12 minutes. Cool for at least 10 minutes before filling.

Make the pie
1. Heat the oven to 350 F.
2. Bring the pumpkin puree to a simmer in a 2-quart saucepan over medium heat. Cook, stirring occasionally, for 2-3 minutes, until slightly thickened.
3. Add the half-and-half, nutmeg, and salt. Stir and return to a simmer. Remove from the heat and cool for 10 minutes.
4. Whisk the brown sugar, eggs, and egg yolk in a large bowl until smooth. Add the pumpkin mixture and whisk until combined.
5. Pour the filling into the warm crust. Bake on the half-sheet pan until the center jiggles slightly but the sides are set, 45-50 minutes.
6. Cool on a rack for at least 2 hours before slicing. The pie can be made and refrigerated up to 2 days ahead; it is best the day after it is made.

Mini Pies
1. Divide the crust mixture evenly among five 5-inch pie tins and bake on a half-sheet pan for 5 minutes. Cool for 10 minutes.
2. Divide the filling among the tins. Bake on the same half-sheet pan until the center jiggles slightly but the sides are set, about 25 minutes. Cool on a rack for 2 hours.
3. Spread 1 tsp brown sugar on each pie. Melt the sugar with a blowtorch to form a crisp top. Cool for 5 minutes before serving.

Source: https://altonbrown.com/recipes/pumpkin-puree/


Struffoli
---------
Struffoli

Ingredients
	8 oz flour
	2 Tbsp sugar
	2 eggs
	Lemon or orange zest (quantity not specified)
	Oil, for frying
	3/4 cup honey, for coating (the source also lists 1 cup honey)

Directions
1. Mix the flour, sugar, eggs, and zest into a dough. Let rest for 30 minutes.
2. Cut strips and roll them into thin ropes. Cut into fingernail-sized pieces; they will puff up while cooking.
3. Working in batches, drop the pieces separately into hot oil so they do not touch. Fry for about 3 minutes, until golden, then drain on paper towels.
4. Heat 3/4 cup honey until it melts slightly. Add the fried pieces and stir with a wooden spoon for 2-3 minutes, until coated.

Note
The source lists both 1 cup honey in the ingredient list and 3/4 cup for coating; the directions specify 3/4 cup.

The Clark Cake
--------------
The Clark Cake

Ingredients

Cake
	1/4 cup butter
	6 eggs, beaten
	1 cup sugar
	1 tsp vanilla
	1 cup sifted cake flour

Chocolate Filling (1/2 recipe)
	2 oz bittersweet chocolate
	1/2 cup sugar
	1 cup milk
	1 tsp butter
	2 Tbsp cornstarch (heaping spoonful)

Chocolate Frosting (3/4 recipe)
	3 oz bittersweet chocolate
	3 Tbsp butter
	1/4 cup milk
	2 cups powdered sugar
	1 tsp vanilla
	1/8 tsp salt

Directions

Cake
1. Preheat the oven to 350 F. Melt the butter and set it aside.
2. In a double boiler, heat the eggs until lukewarm. Add 2/3 cup sugar and beat at medium speed for 7 minutes.
3. Add the remaining 1/3 cup sugar, increase the speed, and beat for 2 more minutes, until the batter falls from a spoon in a continuous, flat ribbon.
4. Add the flour, then the melted butter. Pour the batter into greased and floured cake pans and bake for 30-40 minutes.

Chocolate filling
1. Melt the chocolate and butter in a double boiler. Working over the heat, add the sugar.
2. Use some of the milk to make a cornstarch slurry. Add the slurry and the remaining milk to the chocolate; stir over the heat until thick, 5-10 minutes. Let cool to thicken.
3. Split each cake layer in half and divide the filling into thirds. Spread it between the split layers and between the cake layers.

Chocolate frosting
1. Melt the chocolate and butter in a double boiler.
2. Remove from the heat. Using electric beaters, slowly add the milk and salt.
3. Add the sugar until the frosting stands in thick peaks.

Blackberry-Lime Porch Punch
---------------------------
Blackberry-Lime Porch Punch

Give this punch a kick with a little bourbon, or serve it as is; it is wonderful either way.

Ingredients
	3 cups fresh blackberries
	4 cups chilled sweet tea
	2 cups cold water
	1/2 cup fresh lime juice (from 5 limes)
	4 (12 oz) cans chilled nonalcoholic ginger beer
	1 (16 oz) bag frozen blackberries
	Ice
	Lime slices
	Mint sprigs

Directions
1. Place the fresh blackberries in a large bowl and mash with a potato masher until fully broken. Pour through a fine-mesh strainer into a measuring cup or bowl, pressing the pulp to release the juice. Discard the solids.
2. Pour the blackberry juice into a large (4-6 qt) punch bowl. Stir in the sweet tea, water, and lime juice until well combined. Gently stir in the ginger beer and frozen blackberries.
3. Serve over ice, garnished with lime slices and mint sprigs.

Timing and Yield
	Active time: 20 minutes
	Total time: 20 minutes
	Serves: 12

Make Ahead
Blackberry juice can be made one day ahead and stored in an airtight container in the refrigerator. To prepare the punch ahead, mix in the sweet tea, water, and lime juice on the morning of the party. Add the ginger beer and frozen blackberries just before serving.

Caramel Macchiato
-----------------
Caramel Macchiato

Ingredients
	2 Tbsp caramel sauce, divided
	1 double shot espresso (1/4 cup)
	1/2 cup milk, steamed with foam

Directions
1. Add 1 Tbsp caramel sauce to the bottom of a mug that holds at least 8 oz.
2. Pour the espresso over the caramel, then add the hot milk. Spoon the remaining milk foam on top.
3. Drizzle the remaining 1 Tbsp caramel over the foam and serve promptly.

Yield
1 serving (8 oz)

Notes and Tips
A dedicated milk frother works best, though other methods can foam milk. Skim and whole milk are noted as best for producing plenty of bubbles; whole milk gives a richer, creamier drink. The source recommends fresh caramel sauce for the best flavor.

Homemade Caramel Sauce

Ingredients
	1 cup granulated sugar
	1/3 cup water
	3/4 cup heavy whipping cream
	2 Tbsp salted butter
	1 tsp vanilla extract (optional)

Directions
1. Add the sugar to a heavy-bottomed saucepan and shake the pan so the sugar forms an even layer.
2. Add enough water to moisten the sugar. Do not stir or move the pan after adding the water.
3. Cook over medium heat until the sugar dissolves and turns clear, a few minutes.
4. Increase the heat to medium-high and cook until the caramel turns amber, about 10 minutes.
5. Turn off the heat and immediately add the cream. The caramel may seize; this is expected.
6. Add the butter and stir until smooth. If it remains clumpy, return the pan to low heat and stir gently for a few minutes. Stir in the vanilla, if using.
7. Cool to room temperature, transfer to a jar, and refrigerate.

Caramel Sauce Notes
	Yield: about 1 cup (8 servings). Prep: 5 minutes; cook: 10 minutes; total: 15 minutes.
	To add salt, stir in 1/2 tsp at the end with the vanilla. Unsalted butter may be used; start with 1/8 tsp salt and adjust to taste.
	With a thermometer, target 338-350 F. Above 350 F the caramel can taste burned and bitter; below 338 F it may not caramelize properly.
	Store airtight in the refrigerator for up to 1 month or freezer for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator or in a warm water bath.
	Reheat in a microwave-safe container in 15-second intervals, or in a saucepan over medium-low heat, until warm and pourable.
	To fix crystallized caramel, add 1/4 cup water and bring it back to a boil to dissolve the crystals, then continue cooking.
	Use a heavy-bottomed pan for even heat. Do not stir or move the pan after wetting the sugar until the cream is added. Once the cream is added, the fat helps prevent crystallization.

Classic Sweet Tea
-----------------
Classic Sweet Tea

This sweet tea has a strong flavor without bitterness and is sweet but not cloying.

Ingredients
	12 regular-size tea bags
	1/8 tsp baking soda
	1 qt water
	1 qt ice cubes
	1 1/4 cups Simple Syrup

Directions
1. Place the tea bags and baking soda in a large heatproof measuring cup.
2. Bring the water just to a rolling boil in a saucepan or kettle. Immediately pour it over the tea bags, making sure they are submerged. Cover and steep for 7 minutes.
3. Remove the tea bags without squeezing them and discard.
4. Add the ice and stir until it melts. Stir in the Simple Syrup.
5. Serve over ice.

Timing and Yield
	Hands-on time: 5 minutes
	Total time: 50 minutes
	Yield: 2 qt

Coffee - French Press Cold Brew
-------------------------------
Coffee - French Press Cold Brew

Ingredients
	2 cups dark-roast coffee (140 g), medium-coarse grind
	3 1/2 cups filtered water (840 g)
	8-cup / 34-oz (1 L) French press

Directions
1. Grind the coffee to a medium-coarse grind.
2. Add the grounds and water to the French press and stir several times.
3. Cover the press with plastic wrap or foil and refrigerate for 24 hours. (Alternatively, cover with the top, with the plunger up; this may make the press difficult to fit in the refrigerator.)
4. Fit the lid and slowly press the plunger all the way down. Pour the concentrate into a large jar or pitcher. Store refrigerated for up to 1 week.
5. To serve, dilute 1/2 cup concentrate with 1/2 cup water or milk. For iced coffee, use 1/2 cup concentrate with 1/4 cup water and ice.

Yield and Notes
	Prep time: 24 hours; cook time: 0 minutes.
	Yield: 2 1/2 cups concentrate (5 servings).

Bolognese Meat Sauce AI
-----------------------
Bolognese Meat Sauce AI

Ingredients
	1 Tbsp olive oil
	3 Tbsp unsalted butter
	1 large yellow onion, finely diced
	2 celery stalks, finely diced
	2 carrots, finely diced
	1 lb ground beef chuck (80/20), preferably from the neck
	Salt
	1 cup whole milk
	1/2 tsp ground nutmeg
	1 cup dry white wine (or dry red wine for deeper flavor)
	1 (14 oz) can whole Italian plum tomatoes, crushed by hand (or 28 oz, with half the liquid drained)
	Freshly ground black pepper
	1-2 cups beef or chicken broth, as needed
	Optional: 3 oz minced pancetta, or replace 25-30% of the beef with ground pork

Directions
1. Build the soffritto: Heat the oil and butter in a heavy pot over medium-low heat. Add the onion, celery, and carrot with a pinch of salt. Cook slowly for 10-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until soft, sweet, and translucent but not browned.
2. Add and brown the meat: Add the beef and pancetta, if using. Break it up gently with a wooden spoon and season with salt. Cook over medium heat until the liquid evaporates and the meat begins to brown lightly, about 10-15 minutes.
3. Milk stage: Add the milk and nutmeg. Simmer gently until the milk has fully reduced and only the fat remains, 20-30 minutes. The source says this step protects the meat from acidity and creates the sauce's signature sweetness.
4. Reduce the wine: Add the wine and simmer until it has evaporated, 10-15 minutes.
5. Add the tomatoes: Stir in the tomatoes. When the mixture returns to a simmer, reduce the heat to the gentlest bubble.
6. Cook slowly: Cook uncovered for 3-6 hours, stirring occasionally. Add small amounts of broth as needed to keep the sauce moist but thick. The sauce should become dense and glossy, with a brick-orange rather than red color.
7. Adjust: If too thick, add a splash of broth; if too loose, simmer uncovered longer. For additional richness, swirl in 1 Tbsp butter. Finish with black pepper.

Serving
Serve with fresh tagliatelle or pappardelle, or use as the base for lasagne alla Bolognese. Top with freshly grated Parmigiano-Reggiano.

Matzo Ball Soup
---------------
Matzo Ball Soup

Ingredients

Soup
	1 whole Amish chicken or cut-up chicken pieces with skin
	6-7 medium carrots, peeled and halved
	2 large onions, peeled and halved
	1 bunch dill, washed
	1 bunch parsley, washed
	3-4 parsley roots, peeled and halved if large
	1 Tbsp black peppercorns
	Kosher salt

Matzo balls
	1 cup matzo meal
	4 eggs
	1 tsp baking powder
	1/4 tsp salt
	1/3 tsp pepper
	3 Tbsp melted schmaltz or vegetable oil; the source also says "chicken fat," but its relationship to the alternatives is unclear
	1 tsp minced fresh dill

Directions

Make the soup
1. Rinse the chicken and place it in a stockpot. Cover completely with cold water and bring to a simmer.
2. When scum rises to the surface, let it simmer for about 25 minutes more.
3. Meanwhile, prepare the carrots, parsley root, and onions. The source notes that parsley root is sweet and parsley-like, and that its leaves can also be used as parsley.
4. Pour the broth through a large colander and discard it. Rinse the chicken and pot to remove the scum.
5. Return the chicken to the pot with the vegetables, parsley, dill, salt, and pepper. Cover with cold water and simmer over medium-low heat for about 2 hours.

Make the matzo balls
1. Combine all the matzo-ball ingredients in a large bowl and mix with a spoon until smooth. Refrigerate for 20 minutes.
2. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil. Use a small ice-cream scoop to portion the mixture, roll the portions into balls, and place them on a plate.
3. Carefully drop the balls, one at a time, into the simmering water. Reduce the heat to a simmer, cover, and cook for 35-40 minutes. The source says 25 minutes makes dense matzo balls, while 35-40 minutes makes light, airy ones.
4. Serve immediately with the soup, or cool completely and refrigerate in a separate container.

Serve
Taste the soup and add salt if needed. Serve with one or two matzo balls, some vegetables, chicken on the side, and a sprinkling of dill. To remove some of the fat, refrigerate the soup overnight so the fat solidifies, then remove the solid fat; the source author prefers to leave it in.

Note
The source says the soup and matzo balls can be frozen. To freeze uncooked matzo balls, roll them, freeze on parchment paper, and transfer to a zip-top bag. The source says to cook them as usual when ready to use.

Roast Chicken (Deb)
-------------------
Roast Chicken (Deb)

Ingredients
	1 free-range chicken, 3 1/4-3 1/2 lb (no larger)
	1 1/2 tsp salt, or less if desired
	Freshly ground black pepper
	2-3 sprigs fresh thyme
	2-3 small shallots, peeled, divided
	3-4 inch pieces of lemon or lime peel
	1 cup water
	1 Tbsp butter
	1 Tbsp oil, such as grapeseed
	1 large bay leaf, broken into pieces
	2 garlic cloves, coarsely chopped
	1 tsp finely chopped thyme
	1 cup water or chicken broth
	Wine (amount not specified; for pan gravy)

Directions
1. Preheat the oven to 475 F. Rinse the chicken and clean out the cavity with paper towels. Remove any feather pins. Pat dry and rub all over, including the cavity, with salt and pepper. Put the thyme sprigs, 2 shallots, and citrus peel in the cavity.
2. Tie the legs close to the body and fold back the wing tips, securing with skewers or twine. Lightly oil a shallow roasting pan (13-by-9 inches or smaller) and place the chicken breast-side up.
3. Pour 1 cup water (or the water/broth mixture as described in the source) around the chicken. Add the butter, oil, bay leaf, garlic, chopped thyme, and a coarsely chopped shallot. Roast on the lower oven rack for 15 minutes.
4. Using heavy gloves and a wooden spoon or spatula, turn the chicken breast-side down and roast 15 minutes. Turn it breast-side up and roast another 15 minutes.
5. Lower the oven to 425 F. Turn the chicken breast-side down and roast 15 minutes, then turn it over and roast 10-15 minutes more, until juices run clear at the thigh and wing/breast joint. The source gives an internal temperature of about 175 F.
6. Transfer to a warm platter and rest about 10 minutes without covering with foil.
7. To make gravy, skim excess oil from the roasting pan, leaving about 1 Tbsp if desired. Stir in wine and water or chicken broth, bring to a boil over high heat while scraping up browned bits, then cook 4-5 minutes, stirring. Strain into a warm bowl and serve with the chicken.

Note
The source lists both 1 cup water and 1 cup water or broth, and later says to stir in wine and water or broth for gravy. The exact liquid allocation is unclear.

1. Heat the oil and melt the butter in a skillet over medium-high heat. Add the mushrooms and cook until golden brown, about 4-5 minutes; they do not need constant stirring.
2. Just before the mushrooms are done, add the garlic and a pinch of salt and pepper. Cook until the garlic is golden, about 1 minute.
3. Add the wine. Stir for 1 minute, scraping the bottom of the skillet, or until mostly evaporated.
4. Add the broth, cream, and Parmesan. Stir, then lower the heat to medium so the sauce simmers gently; do not let it boil rapidly, as the cream may split.
5. Stir occasionally and simmer for 2-3 minutes, until thickened. The source notes that it should not become very thick.
6. Stir in the thyme and adjust the salt and pepper to taste. Remove from the heat.
7. Serve over steak or other cooked protein, toss with pasta, stuff into baked potatoes, serve with roast beef, chicken, or pork, or spoon over omelets.

Yield
About 3 cups; enough to generously cover 4-5 pieces of protein or toss with pasta for 3-4 people.

Note
The source refers to numbered notes 1-4 for ingredient details and substitutions, but their text was not included in this file. It also refers to more serving ideas in the original post.

Ranch Dressing
--------------
Ranch Dressing

Ingredients
	Mayonnaise (the source uses Vegenaise as a substitute)
	Plain yogurt or sour cream
	Onion powder
	Garlic powder
	Dried parsley
	Salt and pepper, to taste
	Water or milk, as needed to make a dressing

Directions
1. Decide how much dip or dressing to make.
2. Mix equal parts mayonnaise and yogurt.
3. Add a dash of onion powder, garlic powder, and parsley. For a large batch, use about 1 Tbsp or more of each; for a small batch, use about 1 tsp or more of each, to taste.
4. Add salt and pepper and mix well. At this stage, the mixture is a dip.
5. To make a dressing, add a few tablespoons of water or milk and stir. Add more until it reaches the desired consistency.
6. Serve with vegetables, salad, or ranch chicken.
