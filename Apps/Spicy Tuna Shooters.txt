Spicy Tuna Shooter Recipe
Ingredients

    2 oz fresh sushi-grade tuna, finely diced
    1 tbsp soy sauce
    1 tsp sriracha (adjust for spice preference)
    1 tsp sesame oil
    1 tsp rice vinegar
    1/2 tsp wasabi paste
    1 tbsp green onions, finely chopped
    1 tsp tobiko (fish roe) for garnish
    1/2 tsp sesame seeds
    1 oz vodka or sake (optional, for a boozy kick)
    1 slice cucumber or lime wedge, for garnish

Instructions

    Prepare the tuna mixture: In a small bowl, mix the diced tuna with soy sauce, sriracha, sesame oil, rice vinegar, and wasabi paste. Stir well to coat the tuna evenly.

    Assemble the shooter: Spoon the tuna mixture into a shot glass.

    Add the finishing touches: Sprinkle with green onions, tobiko, and sesame seeds.

    Optional alcohol addition: Pour a small amount of vodka or sake over the tuna mixture for a true shooter experience.

    Garnish & serve: Top with a thin slice of cucumber or a lime wedge. Serve immediately and enjoy!
	
Spicy Tuna Crispy Rice Shooter

    Instead of serving in a shot glass, place the spicy tuna mixture on crispy sushi rice.

    Garnish with jalapeño slices for an extra kick.

2. Tropical Tuna Shooter

    Add diced mango or pineapple to the tuna mix for a sweet contrast.

    Use coconut-infused sake instead of vodka.

3. Korean-Inspired Tuna Shooter

    Mix in gochujang (Korean chili paste) for a deeper, smoky spice.

    Top with kimchi for a tangy crunch.

4. Cucumber Cup Shooter

    Hollow out cucumber slices and use them as edible shot glasses.

    Adds a refreshing, crisp texture.

5. Avocado Tuna Shooter

    Layer the tuna mixture over mashed avocado for a creamy twist.

    Sprinkle with crushed wasabi peas for crunch.

6. Yuzu Citrus Shooter

    Swap rice vinegar for yuzu juice to add a bright, citrusy note.

    Garnish with shiso leaves for a Japanese touch.
