Crab Loaf

1# crabmeat (backfin lump)
3T self rising flour or pancake mix
4 Shakes Worcestershire
1 egg
1T parsley
1T prepared mustard
2T mayonnaise (generones)
3/4 c oil

Put crabmeat in a bowl + pick out shell. Avoid breaking lumps. Add all ingredients except oil & blend gently with 2-pronged fork. Heat oil. Form Crab cakes with ice cream scoop. Fry until golden brown on one side, then turn and Drain on paper towels.
